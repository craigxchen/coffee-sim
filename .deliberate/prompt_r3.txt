## Summary

The bed never moves under the pour because the water↔grain coupling is both miscalibrated and partly wrong in kind. Two mechanisms are at fault: (1) a **soft water↔grain non-penetration "exclusion"** — a partial sphere-sphere contact that is mass-biased so grains barely feel it *and* is exactly the kind of fluid-solid collision that causes **porous locking** (fine fluid wedging in grain voids → bed dilates/locks); and (2) an **implicit drag ~3 orders of magnitude too weak** — the per-substep velocity-blend `β = rate·dt/(1+rate·dt) ≈ 5×10⁻⁴`, far below the grain sleep threshold, so grains read as frozen.

The fix is the textbook **unresolved (semi-resolved) SPH-DEM coupling** (Robinson et al. 2013; Macklin "Unified Particle Physics" 2014): keep PBF for water and rigid XPBD contact for sand-sand, and couple the two phases through (a) the **volumetric solid-fraction field** `α_s` the solver already computes (water density targets `ρ₀·(1−α_s)`, so water packs into pore volume) **backed by an explicit retention floor** so it actually resists free-fall, and (b) a **physically-grounded Darcy drag with an exponential/implicit integrator, formulated as a symmetric pair impulse** strong enough to both set drainage *and* mobilize the bed surface. Only **after** that drag is landed and characterized is the old exclusion removed. Water particles are made genuinely finer than grains, with a **wider coupling kernel** so the porosity/drag field stays smooth.

Coffee pour-over sits at pore Reynolds number ≈ 0.3–12 (Darcian), so the Ergun inertial term is negligible — viscous Darcy drag suffices. At realistic flow the bed is ~3–4× minimum fluidization, so the physical target is **mild grain rearrangement at the pour impingement zone, not a churning bed** — that bounds both the calibration and the verification.

## Key Technical Decisions

- **KTD-1 — Unresolved SPH-DEM coupling, not resolved collision.** Water↔grain interaction is a body force from a locally-averaged porosity field + drag closure (Robinson 2013), **not** sphere-sphere non-penetration (which causes porous locking, spurious collision-pressure, bed dilation). User-specified and literature-standard.
- **KTD-2 — Retention = density-target porosity floor (static) + strong drag (dynamic); no separate body force; remove exclusion only after drag lands.** `α_s` density modulation alone is **not** a hard barrier (a sparse/under-neighbored water particle can advect through, pool below, or ghost in high-α_s zones — Codex r1.1). But a separate `∇α_s` body force is the wrong fix (Codex r2.3/r2.6): it adds another pressure/volume-force channel that fights the buoyancy decoupling and needs its own conservation exception, and a "near-saturation drag limit" can't retain at zero velocity. The reframe: **static retention is moot — at zero relative velocity water is not advecting, so there is nothing to retain.** A resting saturated column is held by PBF **incompressibility** + a **minimum-porosity floor** in the water density target (`target = ρ₀·(1−min(α_s, 1−ε_floor))`, pure PBF, static, momentum-conserving) so water cannot over-pack into grain space; a *moving* wetting front is slowed to the **Darcy velocity by the strong drag** (KTD-3) — percolation, not free-fall. So retention needs **no new force**: density floor + drag. Sequencing: **land the drag first (U3), remove exclusion under it (U2)**, gated by a "no free-fall through a static bed" + bounded-flux test (Codex r1.2/r1.12). If the floor+drag combination empirically still leaks, a *conservative* capillary term is a deferred contingency — not in this plan.
- **KTD-3 — Darcy drag = symmetric capped-pair blend, Jacobi-subiterated (the existing scaffold, correctly sized).** The round-1 "per-pair `exp()` × aggregate normalization" was self-contradictory (Codex r2.1): a per-pair exponential with a receiver-local norm breaks the mirrored equal-and-opposite property. The solver's **existing** drag structure already resolves symmetry + aggregate-bounding + non-overshoot correctly — keep it, fix only the coefficient. Per pair: `s_ij = min(β_max/max(N_i,N_j), harmonic(β_i,β_j)·V_pair·dt)`, applied with the opposite-effective-mass split `s_ij·(m_j/(m_i+m_j))·(vf_j − vf_i)` on i and its exact negation on j, summed over neighbors, **Jacobi-subiterated** K times (refreshing `vel_frozen`). This is (a) **momentum-conserving** — `s_ij` is symmetric (harmonic combiner) and the opposite-mass split is antisymmetric; (b) **aggregate-normalized** — the `β_max/N` cap bounds `Σ_j s_ij ≤ β_max < 1` per subiter, so adding neighbors (wider `h_c`) never over-damps; (c) **implicit / non-overshooting** — each subiter is a bounded convex blend (`s < 1`) toward the relative velocity, and K subiters approach the exponential/implicit limit `1 − (1−s)^K ≈ 1 − e^{−Ks}` *without a literal `exp()`*. Per-grain Darcy rate `β_i = 150·μ·(1−ε_i)²/(ε_i³·d²)` (Kozeny–Carman, already `porosity_drag_factor ∝ d⁻²`; Ergun inertial dropped — Darcian). **The original bug was simply that β was ~1000× too small, so `s` was rate-limited at ~5e-4 instead of cap-limited at `β_max/N`.** Size β (and `β_max`) so a fine/low-permeability bed is cap-limited → strong aggregate drag. **Resolution-normalize** so per-grain total drag is invariant as water is refined (Codex r1.11) — `β` is per-unit-volume and the cap is per-neighbor, so verify invariance with the two-resolution test (U3) before U5.
- **KTD-4 — Mobilization is a separate, *measurable* criterion from stability (Codex r1.5/r2.4).** Viscous drag damps; bed *rearrangement* requires the pour-zone per-frame drag impulse on a grain to **exceed** its yield resistance, measured concretely from quantities the solver already tracks: the XPBD friction budget `friction_mu · normal_impulse[i]` (accumulated contact load) **plus** the grain's submerged weight `(ρ_grain_eff − ρ_w)·V·g`. The gate is a **probe test**: instrument, for pour-zone grains, `|drag_impulse| > friction_mu·normal_impulse + submerged_weight·dt` (yields/moves), and for bulk grains the reverse (stays below fluidization). This makes the inequality measurable, not post-hoc tuning.
- **KTD-5 — Wide coupling kernel `h_c`, water PBF keeps its own radius.** Compute `α_s`/drag porosity over `h_c ≈ 2–3× grain_diameter` for a smooth field; set `cell_size = max(h_c, grain_diameter, h)`, but the **water PBF density solve still distance-culls to its own `h`** (Codex r1.6) — the larger cell only widens the candidate list. Clamp `ε ∈ [0.35, 0.999]`. Instrument avg/max neighbor counts per pass (the wider gather can spike PBF/drag/sort cost).
- **KTD-6 — Reduced units; calibrate by BANDED multi-observable inequalities (Codex r1.8).** No SI system is wired (`KEEP.md §2`). Calibrate the one free drag scale against *several* bands simultaneously: coarse/fine drawdown ratio, bounded pour-zone displacement, a bulk-displacement ceiling, water-retention height, and zero long-run momentum/volume drift — one observable is under-determined. Real-unit conversion is deferred.
- **KTD-7 — Buoyancy separate AND not double-counted with drag (Codex r1.10/r2.6).** Buoyancy stays `−V·∇P` over the PBF `λ` field (density-aware `grain_buoyancy_factor`); drag forces grains only from **relative velocity**. Because KTD-2 drops the `∇α_s` body force, there is **no third pressure channel** to conflict — the only pressure force on grains is buoyancy, the only velocity force is drag, cleanly separated. Gate: a hydrostatic saturated bed at rest has zero net grain acceleration beyond buoyancy balance; a flowing bed receives drag from relative velocity only.
- **KTD-8 — `coupling_scale` lane allocation is explicit; 8-buffer budget held (Codex r1.9/r2.2).** Store exactly: `x = β_i` (per-particle Darcy rate from local ε_i), `y = N_i` (opposite-phase neighbor count, for the `β_max/N` cap). The drag gather reads both sides' `(β, N)` and forms `s_ij = min(β_max/max(N_i,N_j), harmonic(β_i,β_j)·V_pair·dt)` — so it needs neither `α_s` nor `ε` nor a separate aggregate at gather time (ε is consumed only when `β_i` is computed in `compute_coupling_scale`). No pass needs `α_s`, `ε`, and `β` simultaneously; add no bindings to the already-8/8 drag passes; never bit-pack two quantities into one lane.
- **KTD-9 — Normalized, decaying, DRAG-ONLY wake criterion (Codex r1.7/r2.5).** Replace the raw `fluid_impulse > wake_threshold` (scales with dt/subiters/neighbor count) with a **dimensionless** gate (drag impulse relative to grain submerged weight, or local relative-flow speed sustained over a minimum duration) with explicit decay. Crucially, the wake accumulator must be **drag-only** — today `fluid_impulse` is written by *both* `drag_grain` and `buoyancy_grain` (coupling.wgsl:309, 353), so a hydrostatic (zero-flow) saturated bed could wake from buoyancy. Separate the wake signal so buoyancy does **not** contribute to it.
- **KTD-10 — Water finer than grains is realism, sequenced last with drag already resolution-normalized.** `R_w < R_s` improves the look + fluid detail but stresses CFL/kernel normalization (XSPH eruption lesson). Because KTD-3 resolution-normalizes drag, refinement won't change bed forcing; still run the spacing-invariance gate. Keep the count modest; defer the 200k push.

### U4. Reconcile grain sleep/wake (normalized criterion + decay)

**Goal:** The pour mobilizes surface grains; a dry or saturated-static bed settles to rest — via a dimensionless wake gate with explicit decay.

**Requirements:** R5.

**Dependencies:** U3.

**Files:** `src/solvers/xpbd/common.wgsl` (`finalize` sleep/wake; `fluid_impulse` decay), `src/solvers/xpbd/coupling.wgsl` (`fluid_impulse` accumulation from the pair impulse), `src/utils/config.rs`, `tests/xpbd_bed.rs`, `tests/xpbd_coupling.rs`.

**Approach:** Replace raw `fluid_impulse > wake_threshold` with a **dimensionless** wake gate (drag impulse relative to grain submerged weight, or sustained local relative-flow speed) so the threshold is independent of dt/subiters/neighbor count (KTD-9). Crucially the wake accumulator is **drag-only**: today `fluid_impulse` is written by both `drag_grain` and `buoyancy_grain` (coupling.wgsl:309, 353) — split it so **buoyancy does not contribute** (else a hydrostatic, zero-flow saturated bed wakes from buoyancy). Add explicit decay so a saturated, no-longer-flowing bed sleeps. Ensure `rolling_damping` doesn't fully cancel drag-driven surface motion. Dry bed (mixed gates off) untouched.

**Patterns to follow:** the `fluid_impulse` reset→accumulate→consume chain.

**Test scenarios:**

- Active pour: impingement-zone grains stay mobile frame-to-frame; the gate fires on flow, not on transient impulse noise.
- Gate is dt/subiter-invariant: doubling subiters does not change which grains wake.

**Verification:** dry + saturated-static settle; active-pour mobilizes; wake gate resolution/step-invariant.

## Risks & Mitigations

- **Removing exclusion lets water free-fall/pool (Codex r1.1).** Mitigation: land drag first (U3); retention = density-target porosity floor (static PBF) + strong drag limiting advection to the Darcy rate, **no extra body force** (KTD-2); gate on the no-free-fall + bounded-flux/COM tests (U2).
- **Per-particle porosity leaks momentum / drag math inconsistent (Codex r1.3/r2.1).** Mitigation: the symmetric **capped-pair Jacobi** scale `s_ij = min(β_max/N, harmonic(β_i,β_j)·V·dt)` + opposite-mass split (KTD-3) — symmetric by construction, no literal `exp()`/receiver-local norm; the asymmetric-porosity conservation test (U3).
- **Over-damping across many neighbors (Codex r1.4).** Mitigation: the per-neighbor `β_max/N` cap bounds `Σ s_ij ≤ β_max` (KTD-3); the neighbor-count relaxation test (U3).
- **Drag stable but not mobilizing (Codex r1.5).** Mitigation: explicit mobilization inequality (KTD-4) as a calibration target + gate.
- **Under-determined calibration (Codex r1.8).** Mitigation: multi-observable bands (KTD-6).
- **Wider kernel spikes cost / wake gate is unit-dependent (Codex r1.6/r1.7).** Mitigation: water PBF keeps own radius + neighbor instrumentation (KTD-5); dimensionless decaying wake gate (KTD-9).
- **Buoyancy/drag double-count (Codex r1.10).** Mitigation: drag from relative velocity only; hydrostatic-rest gate (KTD-7).
- **Fine-water eruption (Codex r1.11).** Mitigation: resolution-normalize drag in U3 (before U5); spacing-invariance + drag-invariance gates.
- **8-buffer ceiling.** Mitigation: explicit `coupling_scale` vec2 lanes, no new bindings on 8/8 passes (KTD-8).
- **Reduced-unit calibration reads arbitrary.** Mitigation: anchor to the ~3–4× `u_mf` physics; assert bands.

## Sources & Research

External research was load-bearing (KTD-1/3/4/5 and R8's realism bar) and Codex /deliberate round 1 hardened the conservation/sequencing/anti-tunneling specifics:

- **Robinson et al. 2013** (arXiv:1301.0752) — SPH-DEM *unresolved* coupling: porosity-field + Di Felice drag; fluid kernel coarser than ~2× particle diameter for a smooth field. Architectural basis.
- **Implicit Incompressible Porous SPH** (arXiv:2504.07739, 2025) — fluid/solid coexist; implicit (linear-in-v⁺) porous drag — the XPBD-compatible form.
- **Macklin et al. 2014, "Unified Particle Physics"** — PBF + rigid coupling; buoyancy from volume-exclusion/pressure (informs KTD-7).
- **Ergun 1952 / Di Felice 1994 / Gidaspow** — drag closures; viscous `150·μ(1−ε)²/(ε³d²)`; K-C permeability; inertial term negligible at pour-over Re.
- **Coffee puck permeability** (Royal Society Open Science 2025, DOI 10.1098/rsos.252031) — `k ≈ 2.6–35 ×10⁻¹³ m²`, bed Re ≈ 2–12 (Darcian).
- **Fluidization estimate** — V60 `u_s ≈ 3–4× u_mf` → surface rearrangement, not suspension — R8's bar.
- **Codex /deliberate round 1** (`.deliberate/codex_review.md`) — REVISE: pair-symmetric conservation, aggregate normalization, drag-before-exclusion sequencing, α_s retention floor, mobilization inequality, banded calibration, normalized wake, buoyancy/drag decoupling, resolution normalization, sharper anti-tunneling gates.
