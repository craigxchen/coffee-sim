# feat: Pour emission — continuous water injection

## Summary

Make the XPBD solver consume `EmissionInput` to **inject fresh water particles at the kettle
over the brew** (at `flow_rate`, from `kettle_pos`), so water passes *through* the grain bed
continuously and pools as brew in the cup — instead of today's single fixed water column that
drains once. This is the prerequisite that unblocks real-coffee yield/TDS: a fixed column can only
strip `c_sat·V_water` (~1% of dose); continuous pour-through extracts cumulatively across many
water passes.

The design is a **port of the old implementation's emission model** (`crates/sim-wasm/src/engine/
xpbd/inflow.rs` + `crates/sim-core/src/pour.rs`, in git history), which functioned well, **re-seated
onto the new GPU-resident solver and with its bugs fixed** (see Sources & Research for the audit).
The old model was CPU-resident (particle `Vec`s grown by `push`); the new solver has fixed GPU
buffers, so emission becomes a **pre-allocated capacity pool with a growing active count**, with
particles activated CPU-side via `queue.write_buffer` into the active range.

Drained/pooled water stays **live** in the cup and yield/TDS is summed over the cup region (reusing
the Phase-1.5 U7 readout) — no per-frame readback, no atomics, no dropped water. Band calibration is
**deferred** to a follow-up tuning pass; this plan delivers the mechanism + a believable V60 pour.

---

## Problem Frame

`EmissionInput { kettle_pos, flow_rate, pour_angle, event }` exists (`src/emission/mod.rs`) but the
solver ignores it (`fn step(&mut self, dt, _input)` at `src/solvers/xpbd/mod.rs`). All GPU buffers
are sized to a fixed `particle_count` set at build from the seeded lattice, and every pass dispatches
over `groups(self.particle_count)`. There is no runtime particle injection.

Consequences (measured in Phase 1.5): the V60 brew is a one-shot water column; yield ceilings at
~4% of soluble (~1% of total dry mass) and cup TDS at ~0.99%, far under the 18–22% / 1.2–1.4% target
bands — because real yield needs 250 ml flowing *through* 15 g, not a single static charge.

The old MPM/XPBD prototype modeled this correctly (continuous spout inflow + cup drain) and "functioned
well" per the maintainer, but (a) it was CPU-resident and (b) it carries bugs that are benign in MPM
but **fatal in the new PBF hard-incompressibility solve** (a flat slab of coincident emitted particles
erupts). This plan ports the good parts and fixes the bugs.

## Requirements

- **R1 — Continuous injection.** Consume `EmissionInput.flow_rate`/`kettle_pos`/`pour_angle` to emit
  water particles over time at the kettle, at a rate matching the volumetric flow.
- **R2 — Capacity pool + active count.** Pre-allocate GPU buffers to a `capacity`; grow a runtime
  `active_count` as particles are emitted; dispatch passes over `active_count`. Non-pour scenes
  (capacity == seed count) are **byte-for-byte unchanged**.
- **R3 — PBF-safe inlet.** Emission must not inject a density spike that the incompressibility solve
  erupts on. Emitted particles are staggered along the pour axis (sub-frame emission time), not
  coincident in a flat disc. *(Fixes old bug #1.)*
- **R4 — No silent dose loss.** The fractional-particle accumulator persists across pour pauses;
  capacity is sized to the recipe's total water dose; pool overflow logs a loud warning and never
  silently drops water. *(Fixes old bugs #2, #3, #4 — the project rejects silent truncation.)*
- **R5 — Pour recipe layer.** A time-windowed pour script (flow rate + spatial pattern: center /
  spiral / ring / point, with bloom / wait / drawdown via command presence) that a driver samples to
  produce `EmissionInput`. Ports `pour.rs`.
- **R6 — Pour-through brew.** In the V60 pour scene, water emitted at the kettle threads the bed,
  drains through the cone apex, and pools in the cup; yield (cup-region readout, U7) rises with
  continuous pour, materially above the fixed-column ~1%. Deterministic across identical runs.
- **R7 — Conservation under a source.** Emitted water (seeded `c = 0`, `f_w = 1`, `T = pour_t`) is
  accounted: total emitted water mass = in-domain water + absorbed-into-grains, to the documented
  absorption-sink tolerance; the solute inventory `Σ(grain s_f+s_s) + Σ(water c·f_w·V_w)` remains
  conserved by dissolution (emission adds only `c = 0` water, contributing no solute).
- **R8 (deferred)** — calibrate `k0`/`extract_rate`/`c_sat`/pour-schedule to the 18–22% / 1.2–1.4%
  bands. Out of scope here (see Scope Boundaries).

## Key Technical Decisions

### KTD-1 — Capacity pool + active count (not buffer reallocation)
GPU buffers are allocated once to `capacity = seed_count + water_dose_headroom` and never resized;
the live particle set is `active_count ∈ [seed_count, capacity]`, growing as water is emitted.
`params.particle_count` is set to `active_count` each frame (the WGSL kernels already guard
`if i >= particle_count { return }`), and host dispatches use `groups(active_count)`. **Rationale:**
the solver is GPU-resident with fixed-layout storage buffers and a ~200k perf target; reallocating
and re-binding buffers per emission is expensive and churns bind groups. A capacity pool with an
active count is the standard streaming-particle approach and matches the old model's `max_particles`
+ active count. Dormant slots `[active_count, capacity)` are never dispatched, so they need no init.
**Byte-unchanged guarantee:** a scene with no pour declares `capacity == seed_count`, so
`active_count` is constant and every existing suite is identical.

### KTD-2 — CPU-side activation via `write_buffer` (not a GPU emission kernel)
Per-frame emission counts are small (flow_rate · particles_per_volume · dt — a handful). The host
computes the requested count + positions/velocities and writes them into the active range
(`pos[active_count..active_count+requested]`, plus `vel`/`chem`/the moisture lane) with
`queue.write_buffer`, then bumps `active_count`. **Rationale:** matches the old model (CPU `push`),
needs no new compute pass or storage binding (the 8-buffer budget is tight), keeps the emission
geometry/recipe logic in testable CPU code, and a few `write_buffer`s of a handful of vec4s per frame
is negligible. A GPU kernel would only pay off at injection rates far beyond a pour.

### KTD-3 — PBF-safe staggered emission (the critical bug fix)
The old `emit()` placed every particle in a frame at the same `y` (`origin.y − 0.18`), spread only
across the x–z disc by a golden-angle pattern. In MPM (grid transfer, weakly compressible) this slab
was harmless; in the new PBF **hard-incompressibility** solve, N coincident-`y` particles are a local
density spike that triggers the over-correction eruption the project already fought (`project_coffee_sim_rewrite`
convergence-order lesson). **Fix:** stagger each emitted particle along the pour axis by its sub-frame
emission instant — particle `i` of `requested` left the spout at fractional time `i/requested` of the
frame, so its position is `kettle_pos + dir·exit_speed·(i/requested)·dt` (plus the golden-angle disc
offset). This spreads the frame's emission into a physically-correct vertical stream segment instead
of a slab. Keep the deterministic cursor + golden-angle disc (good in the old model).

### KTD-4 — Drain = pool live + cup-region readout (not CPU banking)
Poured water that reaches the cup stays **live** (it settles and pools); yield/TDS is summed over the
cup-cylinder region by the existing Phase-1.5 U7 `sample_extraction`. **Rationale:** the old model
banked drained mass/solute into CPU scalars because it was CPU-resident; on the GPU-resident solver
that would require a per-frame readback of just-drained particles (a realtime stall) or an
atomics-free reduction. Pooling live needs neither, reuses code already written, and never drops
water. **Cost:** `capacity` must hold the full water dose (a bounded, known count from the recipe's
total ml); pooled cup water is mostly quiescent (cheap under the grain sleep / low-velocity regime).

### KTD-5 — Recipe layer drives `EmissionInput`; the solver owns the inflow accumulator
`emission/pour.rs` (ported `PourScript`) is sampled by the driver (example/engine) at the brew time
to produce `EmissionInput`; the solver holds the `InflowState` (fractional accumulator + cursor) and
turns `EmissionInput` into activated particles in `emit()`. **Rationale:** preserves the solver-agnostic
seam (`EmissionInput` is the contract; the recipe is solver-independent) and mirrors the old split
(`pour.rs` recipe vs `inflow.rs` mechanism).

### KTD-6 — Emission seeds the chem/moisture lanes consistently
Emitted water is written with `phase = 0` (water), moisture lane `pos.w = 1.0` (`f_w = 1`, full),
and `chem = (0, pour_t, 0, 0)` (`c = 0`, water temperature = pour temperature). **Rationale:** matches
`seed_chem` for water; emitting at `c = 0` means emission adds no solute, so the U5/U6 conservation
gates are undisturbed (emission only adds dilution/volume). The old hardcoded `92.0` temperature is
replaced by `mats.pour_t`.

## High-Level Technical Design

Per-frame pour pipeline (driver → solver):

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  PS["PourScript.sample(t)\n(emission/pour.rs)"] -->|x,y,flow| DRV["driver maps to\nEmissionInput\n{kettle_pos, flow_rate}"]
  DRV --> STEP["XpbdSolver::step(dt, input)"]
  STEP --> EMIT["emit(): InflowState\naccumulator + golden-angle\n+ y-stagger (KTD-3)"]
  EMIT -->|write_buffer slots\n[active_count .. +req]| POOL[("GPU pool\nactive_count→")]
  EMIT -->|active_count += req\nparams.particle_count = active_count| SUB["substep loop\n(dispatch over active_count)"]
  SUB --> DRAIN["water pools in cup\n(stays live)"]
  DRAIN --> READ["sample_extraction()\ncup-region yield/TDS (U7)"]
```

Capacity / active-count model (KTD-1):

```
build:   capacity = seed_count + dose_headroom        # dose_headroom = 0 for non-pour scenes
         buffers sized to capacity
         active_count = seed_count                     # bed (+ any initial charge)
per frame: requested = inflow.emit(input, dt)          # fractional accumulator → integer count
           requested = min(requested, capacity - active_count)   # clamp + WARN if clamped (R4)
           write pos/vel/chem/moisture for [active_count, active_count+requested)
           active_count += requested
           params.particle_count = active_count        # kernels guard i >= particle_count
           run substep loop over groups(active_count)
```

## Scope Boundaries

**In scope:** continuous water emission from `EmissionInput`; capacity pool + active count; the
PBF-safe staggered inlet; the pour recipe layer; a V60 pour scene + example; conservation accounting
under emission; reuse of the U7 cup yield/TDS readout.

### Deferred to Follow-Up Work
- **Band calibration (R8)** — tuning `k0`/`extract_rate`/`c_sat`/pour-schedule to 18–22% yield /
  1.2–1.4% TDS. The mechanism makes the bands *reachable*; hitting them is an empirical pass.
- **Drain-and-reclaim** — deactivating + slot-reclaiming drained cup water to keep `active_count`
  bounded below the full dose (only needed if capacity = full dose proves too costly; KTD-4 pools
  live instead).
- **CPU drain banking** — exact cup scalars via readback (rejected in KTD-4 for the realtime stall).

### Outside this plan's identity
- **Bloom CO₂ physics** — the `StartBloom`/`StopBloom` events get a minimal hook (pause/scale flow);
  real CO₂ outgassing + freshness is Phase 6 (`solver_xpbd.md` step 6).
- **Recycling/looping brews** — re-emitting drained water (non-physical; re-extraction).
- **Multi-solver emission** — only the XPBD solver; the `EmissionInput` seam stays solver-agnostic.

## Implementation Units

### U1. Capacity pool + active-count refactor

**Goal:** Let the solver hold more particles than it currently simulates — fixed-capacity GPU buffers
with a runtime `active_count` — without changing behavior for non-pour scenes.

**Requirements:** R2.
**Dependencies:** none.
**Files:** `src/solvers/xpbd/mod.rs`, `src/engine/scene.rs` (capacity/dose source),
`src/utils/config.rs` (if a capacity knob is cleaner there), `tests/xpbd_water.rs` or a new
`tests/xpbd_emission.rs` (byte-unchanged assertion).

**Approach:** Introduce `capacity` (buffer sizing) distinct from `active_count` (live set). Size all
per-particle buffers (`pos`, `vel`, `pred`, `vel_smoothed`, `vel_frozen`, `lambda`, `dp`, `c_residual`,
`normal_impulse`, `alpha_s`, `fluid_impulse`, `coupling_scale`, `wet_count`, `diss_neighbors`, `chem`,
`chem_frozen`, `phase`, `sorted_indices`, readback buffers) to `capacity`. Replace per-frame
`self.particle_count` dispatch sizing with `active_count`; set `params.particle_count = active_count`
before each `write_buffer(params)`. Derive `capacity` at build: `seed_count + dose_headroom`, where
`dose_headroom` comes from the scene's declared water dose (e.g. `Scene.water_ml` converted via the
particle volume, or a new explicit `Scene.pour_capacity`/`Config` field) — **0 when the scene has no
pour**, so `capacity == active_count == seed_count` and the layout/dispatch is identical to today.
Grid sizing (`num_cells`) is position/box-derived and unaffected; `cell_start` stays `num_cells+1`;
`sorted_indices` grows to `capacity`. `reset()` restores `active_count = seed_count`.

**Patterns to follow:** the existing buffer-allocation block and `let vec4 = n*16` sizing in
`XpbdSolver::build`; `seed_block` for the seed count; the `groups(self.particle_count)` dispatch sites
in `step()`.

**Test scenarios:**
- Non-pour scene (dam-break, bed-drop, V60 without pour) with `capacity == seed_count`: positions /
  moisture / chem after N steps are byte-identical to the pre-refactor solver (or: existing
  `xpbd_water`/`xpbd_bed`/`xpbd_wetting`/`xpbd_coupling`/`xpbd_geometry`/`xpbd_extraction` suites stay
  green unchanged). Covers R2.
- A solver built with `capacity > seed_count` but `active_count == seed_count` (no emission yet)
  produces identical results to `capacity == seed_count` (dormant slots are inert).
- `reset()` returns `active_count` to `seed_count`.

**Verification:** all existing GPU suites pass unchanged; dormant capacity is provably inert.

### U2. Emission core — `InflowState` + PBF-safe staggered injection

**Goal:** Turn `EmissionInput` into activated water particles each frame, ported from the old
`inflow.rs` with the density-spike and dose-loss bugs fixed.

**Requirements:** R1, R3, R4, R6 (determinism), R7 (emission side).
**Dependencies:** U1.
**Files:** `src/solvers/xpbd/mod.rs` (the `InflowState` + `emit()` + `step()` wiring), possibly a new
`src/solvers/xpbd/inflow.rs` (host module) for the accumulator/geometry, `tests/xpbd_emission.rs`.

**Approach:** Port `InflowState` (fractional accumulator + `cursor`) as host code. `emit(input, dt)`:
(1) `accumulator += flow_rate · particles_per_volume · dt`; `requested = floor(accumulator)`;
`accumulator -= requested` — **do not reset the accumulator when flow is 0** (persist the fraction;
clear only on `PourEvent::Reset`) [fixes bug #2/#3]. (2) Clamp `requested` to `capacity − active_count`;
if clamped, `log::warn!` the dropped count (no silent loss) [fixes bug #4]. (3) For each particle,
compute the golden-angle disc offset (keep the deterministic `cursor`-based spread) AND stagger along
the pour axis by sub-frame time: `pos = kettle_pos + dir·exit_speed·(i/requested)·dt + disc_offset`,
where `dir` derives from `pour_angle` (downward by default) [fixes bug #1 / KTD-3]. (4) `write_buffer`
the new slots: `pos` (with `.w = 1.0` moisture), `vel` (= `dir·exit_speed`), `chem = (0, pour_t, 0, 0)`,
`phase = 0` [KTD-6]. (5) `active_count += requested`; update `params`. Wire `emit()` into `step()`
before the substep loop, gated on `flow_rate > 0`. `PourEvent::Reset` re-seeds; `StartBloom`/`StopBloom`
get a minimal flow-scale hook (stub).

**Execution note:** Add the failing PBF-stability test (emit into a closed box, assert no eruption)
before wiring `emit()` — it is the load-bearing fix.

**Patterns to follow:** the old `crates/sim-wasm/src/engine/xpbd/inflow.rs` `emit()` (accumulator +
golden angle); `write_velocities_for_test`/`write_chem_for_test` for the `write_buffer` mechanics;
`seed_chem` for the water chem lanes.

**Test scenarios:**
- **Rate fidelity:** emitting at a constant `flow_rate` for T seconds activates
  `≈ flow_rate · particles_per_volume · T` particles (accumulator carries the fraction; tolerance ±1).
  Covers R1.
- **Accumulator persists across pauses:** flow → 0 for several frames then resumes → the sub-particle
  fraction is not lost (total over a pulse train matches the integral within ±1). Covers R4.
- **PBF-safe inlet (gate):** continuous emission into a closed box with the full incompressibility
  solve on → positions stay finite, max speed bounded, no eruption (KE bounded); compare against the
  un-staggered (slab) emission which should visibly spike. Covers R3.
- **Capacity clamp warns, never drops silently:** emit past `capacity` → `active_count` saturates at
  `capacity`, a warning is logged, count is exact. Covers R4.
- **Seeding correctness:** emitted particles read back with `phase = 0`, `pos.w = 1.0`, `chem.x = 0`,
  `chem.y = pour_t`. Covers R6/R7.
- **Determinism:** identical pour inputs → identical activated positions/velocities across two runs.

**Verification:** rate matches flow; no eruption at the inlet under the full solve; no silent drops;
deterministic.

## Risks & Dependencies

- **Inlet instability (highest risk).** Even with the y-stagger (KTD-3), a high `flow_rate` can pack
  the inlet. Mitigation: the PBF-safe gate test (U2) runs the full solve; if needed, also cap
  per-frame `requested` (spreading a burst across frames) and/or widen the disc — flagged for
  execution-time tuning.
- **Capacity sizing.** KTD-4 pools live, so `capacity` must hold the full dose; an undersized capacity
  warns + clamps (R4) rather than corrupting, but the brew would under-deliver. The scene must declare
  the dose honestly; the example should assert `active_count` never hit `capacity` unexpectedly.
- **Perf.** Pooling the full dose grows `active_count` toward the perf budget; cup water is quiescent
  (cheap) but counts toward dispatch. Acceptable for the V60 scale; the ~200k perf pass is deferred.
- **Conservation tolerance.** The wetting absorption sink (Phase 1.5) already makes the combined-path
  inventory drift ~1–2%; emission doesn't add solute but the gate must use the same bounded tolerance,
  not assert zero.

6. **"Spiral" is a rosette** (radius locked to angle frequency). → U3 (fix or document; cosmetic).
- **Kept (good):** fractional accumulator, deterministic cursor + golden-angle spread, the `pour.rs`
  recipe layer.

**New-solver context:** `src/solvers/xpbd/mod.rs` (fixed `particle_count`, `groups(particle_count)`
dispatch, buffer sizing `n*16`); `src/emission/mod.rs` (`EmissionInput`/`PourEvent` scaffold);
Phase-1.5 U7 `sample_extraction` cup-region yield/TDS readout (reused for the drain readout);
