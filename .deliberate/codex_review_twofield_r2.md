REVISE

1. **KTD-4 and U6 contradict on pressure correction.** KTD-4 correctly pins `Δv_k = -(dt/ρ_k)∇p` and says the velocity correction must not scale by `φ_k`. U6 then says “`−∇p applies to both fields φ-weighted`.” That reintroduces the exact ambiguity round 1 asked to remove. Fix U6 wording and tests so `φ` weights the continuity/operator mass terms, not the intrinsic velocity correction.

2. **Source-term projection is stated globally but not gated where it matters.** KTD-4 says `s_k` feeds `s_net` in the projection RHS, but U9’s absorption/swelling implementation and tests only gate total volume conservation. There is no test that a local swelling/absorption source produces the correct pore-water displacement through the pressure RHS. Add a U9/U7 source-term manufactured test: prescribed local `s_f, s_s` in a closed bed must generate compensating flux/pressure without density drift.

3. **U6 “frozen skeleton” cannot also prove Skempton B ≈ 1 unless the skeleton has a compressibility model.** An undrained load on a truly kinematically frozen saturated column has no volumetric strain path; B is either trivially forced by boundary reaction or undefined, not a meaningful poroelastic discrimination. Move quantitative Skempton B to U7 with the deformable skeleton, or define an explicit compressible-frame surrogate in U6.

4. **U6’s hydrostatic buoyancy gate conflicts with the frozen-solid scope.** The plan asks a submerged dense block to feel correct buoyant force while U6 freezes the skeleton and omits stress/return-map mechanics. If the block cannot accelerate or deform, the measured “force” must come from a diagnostic reaction, not dynamics. Specify the observable: reaction impulse on a constrained solid body, or defer dynamic buoyancy to U7.

5. **The L0 pressure plan still lacks a precise free-surface pressure operator contract before U4.** U3 measures divergence “interior cells only” and says surface cells are gated in U4, but U4 modifies the pressure solve with surface Dirichlet and bubble rows. That means L0’s final pressure operator differs materially from the one whose assembled `A` passed U3. Add assembled-operator and divergence/volume gates after U4’s surface/bubble rows are inserted.

6. **The bubble constraint is underspecified as a linear-system augmentation.** “One aggregate constraint row folded into the coarse solve” is not enough: the plan needs to define whether the bubble volume constraint couples to pressure as an extra Lagrange multiplier, a pressure offset, or a coarse-cell source, and how that row is represented in the fine cleanup. Otherwise U4 can pass by coarse-only volume hacking while fine Jacobi erodes the constraint.

7. **The operator tests omit the actual face-velocity interpolation issue called out by the Rhie-Chow corollary.** KTD-2 says drag and solids-pressure contributions must consistently enter the velocity feeding divergence, but U3/U6 only gate `A = -D M^-1 G` and mixture divergence. Add a U6/U7 checker that the face velocities used in `D(φv)` include the same drag, pressure, solids-pressure, and boundary terms as the momentum update.

8. **The drag split gate does not include projection-induced slip changes.** U6 tests slip decay under timestep refinement, but the hard case is drag + pressure projection + variable `φ` + boundary outflow in one coupled loop. Add a closed manufactured two-phase column where the reference is subcycled drag/projection together, not drag decay alone.

9. **Stress partition audit still leaves the over-packing guard ambiguous.** KTD-5 says the guard lives “inside the contact/effective partition,” but U5 describes a grid-level solids-pressure term that “resists further compaction in the two-field update.” U7 must audit that this solids-pressure contribution is included exactly once in effective stress and is not also projected as pore pressure or added as an independent volume constraint.

10. **Parallel U5/U6 sequencing conflicts with the stated rung discipline.** The ladder says L1 does not start until L0 passes, and L2 follows L1, but later U6 runs in parallel with U5 immediately after L0. That may be intentional, but the document currently describes both a strict rung ladder and cross-rung concurrency. Clarify that U6 is an early L2 risk spike with its own halt rules and that U9 remains the actual L2 ladder gate.

11. **U8 spatial convergence can conflict with fixed-budget Jacobi.** The plan asks a 3-resolution Richardson/GCI sweep while intentionally using a fixed small number of fine Jacobi sweeps. If the iteration count is fixed in absolute sweeps, iterative error usually grows relative to truncation error under refinement. Predefine whether sweeps scale with grid resolution for the offline convergence study, or the gate may fail for a reason unrelated to spatial discretization.

12. **The L3 200k performance gate is under-constrained by particle mix.** “200k particles” can mean 188k water + 12k solids or a much more expensive solid-heavy scene with SVD. Since the plan relies on low solid counts to bound SVD cost, U8 must pre-register the exact 200k composition, grid resolution, substep count, and scene duration used for the ≤33 ms median gate.
