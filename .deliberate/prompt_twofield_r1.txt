# feat: Unified two-field multiphase solver (incompressible water + elastoplastic porous bed)

## Summary

Build a new grid-based solver under `src/solvers/` — a two-velocity-field, APIC-transfer, constraint-incompressible multiphase method — that handles all three target interactions in one method: water↔water (incompressible, with impact cavities), granular bed↔itself (Drucker-Prager elastoplasticity, angle of repose, tamping memory), and water↔bed with porosity (Darcy percolation at physical infiltration rates through a deformable, swelling bed, with pour craters). The work is structured as a validation ladder of rungs (L0 water → L1 bed → L2 percolation → L3 full coupling), each rung with quantitative exit gates. This is a pure bet on the novel architecture: a failed gate stops the ladder for redesign — there is no pre-committed fallback path.

---

## Problem Frame

Two complete prior attempts failed in complementary halves, and four independent attacks on the pressure solve failed for the same root cause:

- **main (MPM + global Poisson):** plausible bed deformation, but (a) the pressure solve consumes ~70% of a ~30 ms frame and sparse RBGS (PR #18, 1.93×) is its practical ceiling — Jacobi-CG, adaptive iterations, and indirect dispatch all failed because the real bug is operator inconsistency (compact stride-1 solve vs central stride-2 divergence, A ≠ D·G → a divergence floor no solver beats); (b) water pools above the bed unreasonably long because Darcy resistance is formulated as a divergence penalty in the pressure RHS, which imposes a pressure-jump entry barrier at the bed surface — inconsistent with the correct Stokes–Darcy interface conditions (flux + stress continuity, not pressure continuity); (c) the bed is a fixed scaffold with a scalar porosity — no granular mechanics at all.
- **rewrite (XPBD/PBF):** percolation, wetting/swelling (volume-conserving), and granular statics all work and are test-gated — but position projection derives velocity *after* the constraint solve, so impact momentum dies in the density projection. Both impact-coupling attempts (water→grain committed-dormant; water↔water reverted in `4dca52f`) confirmed there is no contact-pressure layer to patch. No craters, no cavities, no tamping memory.

No published method covers all three interactions in real time: mixture-MPM (Tampubolon 2017, Gao 2018) is offline and pools on dry beds (drag activates only after interpenetration); Genesis-style couplers average velocities and forbid percolation; GIC (SIGGRAPH Asia 2025) has the right absorption lifecycle but runs on an 80-core CPU. The two-velocity-field + cheap-incompressibility + cavity combination is the open, publishable core.

---

## Requirements

**Interactions**

- R1. Water behaves incompressibly (visual density band per existing water gates) and carries impact momentum: a fast jet into a pool produces a measurable, transiently sustained cavity; a center pour craters a granular bed.
- R2. The bed is a pressure-dependent elastoplastic granular material: tunable angle of repose reaching ≥30° (coffee band 32–48°), column collapse with plausible runout, static rest without creep, and plastic compaction memory under repeated load (tamping persistently raises packing fraction).
- R3. Water percolates through the bed as relative two-phase flux: Darcy flux scales with K(φ)·head and grind d², infiltration at the free-water/bed interface proceeds at physical rates (no artificial entry barrier), ponding forms only when pour demand exceeds bed capacity and drains on the calibrated timescale after pouring stops.
- R4. Wetting is conserved and mechanical: absorbed water volume transfers 1:1 into grain moisture, grains swell (+15–17% diameter at saturation) with effective mass for momentum, and local porosity/permeability evolve with swelling and compaction.

**Conservation and correctness (hard constraints)**

- R5. Volume (water + absorbed + pore) and momentum are conserved within documented float budgets, including the long-run saturated-tail regime; solute/extraction hooks are out of scope but the conservation pattern must not preclude them.
- R6. Deterministic stepping given state + input + seed; no non-finite state ever; the `Solver` trait getters never stall the GPU.

**Performance and platform**

- R7. The frame contains no converged global Poisson solve. Incompressibility cost is bounded: one coarse-grid solve (≤ ~1/64 of fine cell count) plus a fixed small number of fine local iterations.
- R8. Dispatches/frame and max-storage-buffers-per-entry-point are first-class budgets, recorded from rung L0 onward via `Solver::profile()`. Sparsity uses over-dispatch + early-out, never `dispatch_workgroups_indirect`.
- R9. Scaling is ~linear in particle count to 200k (ramped: measured at each rung; 200k is the L3 target, not an L0 gate), and real-time is a gated absolute number, not just structure: ≤33 ms/frame median at 200k particles on the reference device (Apple M5) is the L3 exit gate — a perfectly linear 90 ms/frame solver fails the program. Each earlier rung pre-registers a wall-clock ceiling for its representative scene when its gate tests are written; exceeding a ceiling is a halt condition alongside the physics gates. WGSL compiles unmodified for native + web; WebGPU baseline limits except the storage-buffer request.

**Integration**

- R10. The solver is one complete entry behind the `Solver` trait (`src/solvers/base.rs`): six methods, canonical `ParticleBuffers` layout, `Metrics`/`Profile`, three-edit registration (`src/engine/solvers.json`, `SolverId`, `build_solver`). Existing scenes, `src/models/` physics laws, and the V60 geometry are reused, not duplicated. The XPBD solver and existing tests remain untouched and green.

## Key Technical Decisions

- KTD-1 — **Two velocity fields per grid node (water + solid), APIC particle↔grid transfers.** Percolation requires co-located phases at *different* velocities (relative Darcy flux); contact requires momentum exchange on a shared grid. One field per phase satisfies both; velocity-averaging couplers (Genesis) are structurally rejected. APIC (per-particle affine matrix C) is the momentum carrier that makes cavities — its absence is precisely why XPBD craters failed. Per the v1 data-path lesson (`docs/issues/010-separate-water-bed-data-paths.md`), water and solid particles occupy separate buffer ranges with phase-specific dispatches, not a shared branchy buffer.
- KTD-2 — **Incompressibility = coarse-grid pressure seed + fine local Jacobi cleanup, on one discretely consistent operator family.** The coarse solve (e.g. 1/4³ resolution) kills the low-frequency modes local iteration can't reach (this is the unbuilt PB-MPM 3D-convergence fix — the novel core); a fixed budget of fine Jacobi sweeps kills high-frequency residual. No converged Poisson (main's graveyard), no EOS-only fallback for the shipped path (an adaptive-Tait weak-compressibility predictor, c_s ≈ 10·v_max, is permitted as a pre-projection stabilizer if implementation finds it helps). **Operator consistency is a stated gate, not an aspiration:** divergence, gradient, and both grids' Laplacians come from one MAC-consistent family, the coarse operators are consistent restrictions of the fine ones, and rung L0 must measure the post-projection divergence and show it decays below a pre-registered tolerance as the iteration budget grows — a floor that plateaus above tolerance is the failure, and it is the exact signature that killed main's adaptive-RBGS, so it must be demonstrably absent. Two-field corollary from production Euler-Euler practice (the multiphase Rhie-Chow lesson, Cubero 2007 / Ferreira 2021): whatever velocity feeds the divergence must carry the drag and solids-pressure contributions consistently with the momentum update, or φ_s develops spurious oscillations even when the pressure operators themselves are consistent.
- KTD-3 — **Darcy drag is a velocity body force folded semi-implicitly into the grid update — never a divergence penalty.** Form: drag rate β(φ) from `src/models/permeability.rs` (Kozeny–Carman), applied per node between the two velocity fields via the analytic exponential (Laibe–Price) update `Δv⁺ = Δv·exp(−β·dt)` — exact for any stiffness, unconditionally stable at coffee's worst-case density ratio (ρ_f/ρ_s ≈ 0.83), momentum-conserving by construction (mass-weighted pair update). Main's divergence-penalty formulation is recorded as a named anti-pattern: it blocks interface entry flux and is the root cause of the stuck water layer. This per-node analytic fold is the same structure production Euler-Euler codes use for stiff interphase drag (Fluent PC-SIMPLE's Partial Elimination Algorithm; MFiX-Exa reports drag iteration counts dropping from 50+ to 3–5 with it) — a 2×2 closed-form inversion, zero global communication. Two production refinements are adopted: (1) **drag-law blending** — Kozeny-Carman/Ergun is the packed-bed law and over-predicts drag in dilute regions (pour entry, splashed grains); blend toward a dilute-suspension law (Wen-Yu form) below φ_s ≈ 0.2 with a smooth transition (the Gidaspow/Huilin-Gidaspow pattern every TFM code ships); (2) if residual added-mass jitter appears despite the exponential fold, the standard stabilizer is a **virtual-mass term** (C_vm ≈ 0.5) added symmetrically to both phases — a named lever, default off.
- KTD-4 — **Infiltration interface physics: mixture incompressibility + wetting-front suction + phase-change absorption.** (a) The incompressibility constraint is the mixture form ∇·(φ_f·v_f + φ_s·v_s) = 0 (locally averaged; reduces to ∇·(φ_f·v_f) = 0 over a rigid skeleton), with the pressure gradient applied to BOTH grid velocity fields, φ-weighted (Gao 2018's locally-averaged form) — so pore water generates no artificial pressure in a static bed, submerged grains feel buoyancy through the momentum equation (not just constitutively), and a compacting or swelling skeleton correctly displaces pore water instead of silently creating or destroying volume. (b) A capillary suction body force acts on water at the unsaturated wetting front (Green–Ampt; ψ_f ≈ 50–110 mm physical for ~400 µm media, mapped to reduced units), gated by a hydrophobic→wetted transition so the bloom-phase entry delay falls out naturally. (c) Absorption is a moisture phase-change at contact (GIC-style): grid-projected moisture deficit drives transfer from water particles into per-grain moisture, reusing the existing volume-conserving wetting law (`src/models/wetting.rs`) — absorption starts on contact, not after interpenetration.
- KTD-5 — **Bed constitutive model: Klar et al. 2016 Drucker-Prager return map** (3-branch, log-strain, one 3×3 SVD per solid particle per substep) with saturation-dependent cohesion from `src/models/cohesion.rs` (Bishop-style effective stress) and a compaction cap surface for tamping memory (plastic volumetric strain persists and feeds packing fraction → permeability). The tensile-apex branch is what lets crater walls stand and then collapse.
- KTD-6 — **Cavity sustain via constraint bubbles** (Goldade & Batty 2017): free-surface cells are tracked; enclosed air pockets get one aggregate volume constraint each in the pressure solve, so the pour cavity doesn't instantly re-level. Rung L0 ships the free-surface treatment plus at minimum the single-bubble case (the pour cavity is one pocket); generalization to arbitrary pocket counts may defer.
- KTD-7 — **GPU layout discipline:** P2G scatter uses fixed-point `atomic<i32>` accumulation (KEEP.md §3 pattern, FP_SCALE re-validated against the velocity cap with documented overflow headroom); bind groups are split by pass family (transfers / pressure / plasticity / coupling) per `docs/issues/003-split-mpm-bind-groups.md`; every entry point fits the device's storage-buffer request. **Discrepancy to resolve in U1:** `src/utils/gpu.rs` currently requests exactly 9 storage buffers (`NEEDED_STORAGE_BUFFERS`) while AGENTS.md sanctions 16 — the scaffold unit raises the request only if the two-field grid genuinely needs it and verifies browser grants; grid fields are packed into vec4 lanes first.
- KTD-8 — **Porosity and permeability are local, evolving fields:** φ_s from solid-phase P2G mass (smooth via the transfer kernel), K(φ) per node via Kozeny–Carman, updated by swelling, plastic compaction, and (later, deferred) fines. A configured global porosity scalar — main's approach — is rejected; measured intra-bed K heterogeneity spans an order of magnitude.
- KTD-9 — **The validation ladder is the project spine and the execution posture.** Each rung's gate tests are written before that rung's physics (test-first at the gate level, mirroring the analytic-test doctrine: discrete-recurrence references, CPU twins of GPU kernels, set-invariant assertions, headless GPU gating, no `#[ignore]`). A failed gate halts the ladder for redesign — by explicit decision there is no pre-committed fallback architecture. To make the halt implementable rather than a tuning purgatory, each rung declares a knob grid when its gate tests are written (for U3: coarse ratio {1/4³, 1/8³} × a stated fine-sweep range × Tait predictor on/off); exhausting the grid without a pass IS the halt condition, and reaching for any knob outside the grid is a redesign decision, not a retry.
- KTD-10 — **Exposure:** native examples and the registry first; the web frontend (`src/web.rs` hard-codes `XpbdSolver`) is not generalized in this plan — deferred to follow-up work once L3 passes.

## High-Level Technical Design

Per-substep pipeline (the two-field core):

```mermaid
flowchart TB
  A[P2G: water particles to grid field W<br/>fixed-point atomics, APIC] --> C
  B[P2G: solid particles to grid field S<br/>+ stress divergence from DP return map] --> C
  C[Grid forces: gravity, suction at wetting front] --> D
  D[Drag fold: Laibe-Price exponential pair update<br/>between fields W and S per node]
  D --> E[Coarse-grid pressure solve<br/>restricted operators, bubble constraints]
  E --> F[Fine local Jacobi sweeps + projection<br/>on the mixture divergence, -grad p to both fields]
  F --> G[G2P: APIC gather per phase<br/>update C matrices, advect]
  G --> H[Particle ops: plastic return map SVD,<br/>moisture phase-change, swelling]
```

Validation ladder (rungs = delivery milestones; each gate is a test binary that must be green before the next rung starts):

```mermaid
flowchart TB
  L0[L0 water: APIC + coarse-seed incompressibility + cavity<br/>GATES: divergence decays below pre-registered tolerance,<br/>momentum conserved, jet cavity forms, budgets recorded]
  L1[L1 bed: Klar DP + cap<br/>GATES: repose >= 30 deg tunable, collapse runout,<br/>no static creep, tamping raises packing persistently]
  L2[L2 percolation, rigid bed first<br/>GATES: Darcy flux scaling, pond forms/drains in band,<br/>volume+momentum conserved incl. swelling, long-run tail]
  L3[L3 full coupling: deformable saturated bed<br/>GATES: pour craters bed, swelling lifts, combined conservation,<br/>200k scaling probe ~linear]
  L0 --> L1 --> L2 --> L3
```

Sequencing note: U6 (L2's rigid-skeleton drag/projection half) deliberately runs concurrently with rung L1 — it needs only static solid mass on the grid, not plasticity — so a failure in the two-field coupling surfaces before U5's SVD/return-map investment is sunk. The L2 ladder gate (U9's ponding/drain bands) still requires both rungs' machinery.

Grid-level over-packing guard (the TFM solids-pressure pattern): φ_s is hard-clipped at the packing limit and a solids-pressure term that diverges as φ_s → φ_max resists further compaction in the two-field update — the particle-level return map alone cannot guarantee this on the grid.
- **Execution note:** characterization-first for the SVD/return-map math — the CPU twin and its unit tests precede the WGSL port.
- **Patterns to follow:** Klar et al. 2016 / warp-mpm `sand_return_mapping` (external reference, cited in Sources); existing xpbd bed gate structure in `tests/xpbd_bed.rs`.

- **Verification:** all L1 gates green; repose/collapse metrics recorded.

### Rung L2 — percolation through a (first rigid, then swelling) bed

### U6. Two-field Darcy drag and porosity-weighted incompressibility (rigid skeleton)

- **Goal:** Relative two-phase flux through a static bed obeys Darcy's law with no artificial entry barrier — the formulation half of L2's percolation.
- **Requirements:** R3 (flux scaling, entry), R5
- **Dependencies:** U3, plus a thin slice of U2's solid-phase P2G (static seeded grains contributing mass to φ_s only — no stress, no return map). Deliberately NOT dependent on U5: the rigid-skeleton regime needs no plasticity, so the program's second-riskiest novelty (two-field drag + mixture projection) is gated immediately after rung L0, in parallel with U5, before the plasticity investment is sunk.
- **Files:** create `src/solvers/twofield/coupling.wgsl`; modify `pressure.wgsl` (porosity-weighted divergence), `mod.rs`; test `tests/twofield_coupling.rs`.
- **Approach:** φ_s per node from solid P2G mass; β(φ) via `src/models/permeability.rs` mirrored in WGSL ("mirrors models::" comment convention); Laibe–Price exponential drag pair update between the two node velocities (mass-weighted, momentum-conserving); the mixture constraint ∇·(φ_f·v_f + φ_s·v_s) = 0 (with v_s = 0 while the skeleton is frozen) replaces plain divergence in the projection, and −∇p applies to both fields φ-weighted. Column-outflow boundary at the container base for the drained-flux tests. The bed is kinematically frozen in this unit (rigid-skeleton regime) so drag and pressure formulation are isolated from plasticity.
- **Execution note:** test-first; the Darcy-scaling and momentum gates precede the coupling kernels.
- **Patterns to follow:** `tests/xpbd_coupling.rs` (Darcy drawdown scaling tests).
- **Test scenarios:** drained flux through a static saturated column scales ∝ K(φ)·head and ∝ d² across a grind sweep (ordering + ratio bands); water entering the bed surface sees no pressure-jump barrier — entry flux matches the head-driven Darcy prediction within a stated band (the anti-divergence-penalty gate); drag pair update conserves combined momentum per node exactly; a settled saturated column stays settled long-run (drag does not pump energy); a submerged dense block in hydrostatic water feels the correct buoyant force, and an undrained vertical load on a frozen saturated column raises pore pressure (mixture-constraint discrimination gates); the U3 divergence-decay gate re-runs on a variable-φ saturated column against the mixture divergence (operator consistency in the φ<1 regime — the regime the novelty claim lives in); free-water region (φ_f = 1) behavior is byte-identical to U3 results (coupling terms vanish where there is no bed).
- **Verification:** Darcy-scaling and entry-flux gates green; anti-pattern check — no Darcy term anywhere in the pressure RHS.

### U9. Infiltration interface and conserved wetting: suction, bloom, absorption, swelling

- **Goal:** Infiltration proceeds at physical rates with the two-regime entry behavior, and wetting is volume-conserving with mechanical swelling — completing the L2 rung.
- **Requirements:** R3 (infiltration rates, ponding), R4, R5
- **Dependencies:** U6
- **Files:** modify `src/solvers/twofield/coupling.wgsl`, `transfers.wgsl`, `mod.rs`; test `tests/twofield_infiltration.rs`.
- **Approach:** Wetting-front suction body force at unsaturated-front nodes (Green–Ampt magnitude from the calibration table, mapped to reduced units), gated by per-grain wetted state (hydrophobic→wetted transition with a time/contact threshold → the bloom delay); GIC-style moisture phase-change using `src/models/wetting.rs` demand curves (volume 1:1 into grain moisture; effective mass and diameter swell per the existing law); swelling and compaction feed the local φ/K fields from U6. Phase-selective boundary at the V60 filter surface: the solid field sees a wall, the water field sees open outflow per the filter SDF species mask — the pond-drain gate cannot pass without it. This is exactly Fluent's porous-jump membrane condition (Δp = (μ/α·v_n + C₂·½ρv_n²)·Δm applied to the water field's normal flux; wall for the solid field), so the filter's flow resistance is a calibrated Darcy+Forchheimer face coefficient, not ad hoc. If the wetting-front suction nonlinearity destabilizes the explicit update at the dry limit, the named remedy is L-scheme stabilization (add L·(s_new − s_old) with L ≥ half the retention curve's Lipschitz constant — unconditionally convergent exactly where Newton-style updates fail).
- **Execution note:** test-first; the conservation gates precede the interface kernels.
- **Patterns to follow:** `tests/xpbd_wetting.rs` (volume/momentum conservation incl. effective mass, dense-scene and saturated-tail variants).
- **Test scenarios:** infiltration into a dry wettable bed starts on contact (no interpenetration delay) and early-time rate exceeds the bare-Darcy rate when suction is enabled (Green–Ampt regime A/B); with the bloom gate enabled, a dry bed shows a seconds-scale entry delay then accelerates (two-regime discrimination); pour at demand > capacity ponds, pond depth stabilizes, and after pour stop the pond drains within the calibrated band (medium-grind scenario mapped to reduced units); total volume (free + absorbed + pore) conserved exactly over the run including a ≥2000-step saturated tail; absorption conserves momentum under the effective-mass convention; grains swell to the calibrated magnitude and the local K drops accordingly (monotonic gate); water drains through the filter boundary while grains are retained (phase-selective boundary gate).
- **Verification:** the ponding/drain band test is the L2 ladder gate; conservation gates green.

## Risks & Dependencies

- **Coarse-seed convergence is the novel bet (highest risk).** The PB-MPM 3D-convergence fix this implements has no published reference implementation. Mitigation: it is the *first* gated unit after transfers (U3), the gate is quantitative (divergence-floor curve), and by explicit decision a gate failure halts for redesign rather than silently shipping a compressible-looking fluid. Watch specifically for coarse/fine operator inconsistency reproducing the A≠D·G floor — the gate is designed to catch exactly this.
- **Added-mass instability at ρ_f/ρ_s ≈ 0.83.** Explicit drag coupling diverges at near-unity density ratios; the Laibe–Price exponential fold is the mitigation (exact for linear drag at any stiffness). If the drag↔projection Lie split still shows coupling artifacts, the escalation ladder is named and bounded: Strang splitting (drag/2 → project → drag/2, O(dt²) splitting error at ~2× cost), then 2–5 Aitken-relaxed sub-iterations of the pair (the partitioned-FSI standard), then the virtual-mass term from KTD-3 — all short of redesign. Residual risk: nonlinear (Forchheimer) corrections at high flux, deferred unless gates demand them.
- **Storage-buffer ceiling.** Two grid velocity fields + mass + pressure + divergence + φ + flags is buffer-hungry; the device currently grants 9/stage. Mitigation in U1: vec4 packing first, raise the request second, verify web. If a pass genuinely cannot fit, split it (dispatch budget absorbs it — that's what the budget gate is for).
- **SVD-per-particle cost in WGSL.** One 3×3 SVD per solid particle per substep is the R1 cost center. Solid counts are small (~12k grains vs 100k+ water), so this is bounded; the scaling probe separates phase costs to verify.
- **Fixed-point P2G contention/overflow.** Known-hot in dense regions (`docs/issues/004-reduce-p2g-atomic-contention.md`); FP_SCALE headroom is re-validated in U2 and coupled to the velocity cap. Workgroup-local accumulation is the named lever if contention shows up in the per-pass timings.
- **Calibration-unit mapping.** The physical bands (K, ψ_f, drain times) must be mapped into reduced units (KEEP.md §2) before gates freeze; KEEP warns the length scale is baked into permeability timescales. The mapping is an explicit early task inside U6, not an afterthought.
- **Dependency:** no new crates anticipated; everything builds on wgpu 29 / existing utils. Any SVD reference implementation is ported, not added as a dependency.
