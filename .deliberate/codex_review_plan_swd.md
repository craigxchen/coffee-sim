REVISE

The central transfer lever is sound, but the plan overclaims the drip-stir guard and has a few concrete implementation holes.

PIC fraction as grid-XSPH is a valid working model: XPBD’s `xsph` does `vi + c * Σ(vj - vi)W` in [water.wgsl](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/src/solvers/xpbd/water.wgsl:226), and `v = mix(v_own, v_grid, c)` is the grid analog. `v_own = vel[p] + gravity * dt` is also the right ballistic reference; the current open-base path already uses that recurrence in [transfers.wgsl](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/src/solvers/twofield/transfers.wgsl:165). But the plan must be precise on affine damping: do not literally multiply `C` by `(1 - c)`. Since `c=1` in the bulk, that would zero bulk APIC and break the “reduces to current behavior” claim. The correct pattern is damping amount proportional to preserve, e.g. `C *= 1 - k * (1 - c)`, matching the prototype at [transfers.wgsl](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/src/solvers/twofield/transfers.wgsl:216).

`trace(C)` is available, but the plan overstates what it proves. The gather already forms `B` in [transfers.wgsl](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/src/solvers/twofield/transfers.wgsl:182), and the correct divergence signal is `dinv * (b0.x + b1.y + b2.z)` before extra damping. The plan’s “trace of B” wording is physically wrong; `B` is not the gradient until multiplied by `4/h²`. More importantly, this `g2p_water` runs after the pressure `project` pass in [mod.rs](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/src/solvers/twofield/mod.rs:2648). A merging drip can therefore read near-zero or even positive divergence after projection, exactly where the plan wants “compressive ⇒ c→1”. Under-converged Jacobi also makes the sign noisy. So the sign is a useful input, not a structural guarantee against the old `main` failure.

The R5 negative control is the right idea, but not sufficient as written. The old failure really is density/support preserve in the cup, visible in `main`’s block around `support_ratio`, `dense_support_ratio`, and preserve caps. But “density-only MUST exhibit stir” is brittle unless the control uses the same tuned curve and an old-main-like sustained sparse-drip scene. Add a classification/telemetry gate: during slow drip into a settled cup, particles crossing into the pool must have high `c` before KE/circulation assertions are considered meaningful.

The pressure split is acceptable only if the plan downgrades its splash claims. The origin explicitly says the prototype improved static/settled water but impact still mushed, and likely needs density-target pressure too in [the origin doc](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/docs/brainstorms/2026-06-18-twofield-water-splash-dynamics-requirements.md:91). The reviewed plan acknowledges that risk later, but U2/U4 still claim free/impact splash and production enablement. Transfer-only can restore free-flight liveliness and reduce over-damping; it should not promise a full impact crown into a too-compressible pool.

There is also a concrete packing bug. U1 says to pack `c_surface`, `dens_lo`, `dens_hi`, plus an enable flag into `box_min.w`, `box_max.w`, and `gravity.w` in [the plan](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/docs/plans/2026-06-18-001-feat-twofield-surface-weighted-dissipation-plan.md:82). That is four values into three lanes. The divergence scale mentioned later also needs definition or derivation. The three lanes are genuinely unused today in [mod.rs](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/src/solvers/twofield/mod.rs:1484), but the plan needs an explicit encoding.

Constraints otherwise look feasible. No new storage buffer is needed for the ballistic path. A per-particle branch in `g2p_water` is Tint-safe because this module has no barrier. FP headroom is correctly coupled to `max_speed` in [common.wgsl](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/src/solvers/twofield/common.wgsl:117), and the existing overflow probe is the right base. Perf risk is small: `g2p_water` is only 4.4% of the 25.4 ms 200k frame in [PERF_NOTES](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/docs/PERF_NOTES.md:93), with no new passes.

**Specific REVISE Items**
- Specify `div = (b0.x + b1.y + b2.z) * 4/(h*h)` computed before any new affine damping; do not call raw `B` a velocity gradient.
- Replace the drip-stir guarantee with a more robust rule: divergence sign plus a pool-merge/relative-approach or surface/near-air discriminator. Do not rely on post-projection divergence sign alone.
- Fix affine damping wording and formula: bulk `c=1` must preserve today’s `C = B * dinv * (1 - pic_blend)`.
- Resolve Params packing: three unused `.w` lanes cannot hold floor, two density thresholds, enable, and divergence scale unless one is encoded or derived.
- Reconcile default-off/byte-identical behavior with the current `FLIP_PROTO: true` block in [transfers.wgsl](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/src/solvers/twofield/transfers.wgsl:148); define the exact baseline being pinned.
- Scope R1/R12 to “transfer-side splash/liveliness improves vs disabled baseline”; leave full pool-impact crown as pressure-follow-up dependent unless it actually passes.
- Make R5 control non-gameable: same scene, same tuned density curve, compression gate disabled, plus classification evidence that merging drip particles get high `c` only with the revised guard.
- Clarify clamp relaxation: current `params.max_speed` is global and also used by `drag_fold` and solid update, so either introduce a separate water/splash cap or acknowledge and gate the global side effects.
