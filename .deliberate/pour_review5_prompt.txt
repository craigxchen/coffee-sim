# feat: Pour emission — continuous water injection

## Summary

Make the XPBD solver consume `EmissionInput` to **inject fresh water particles at the kettle
over the brew** (at `flow_rate`, from `kettle_pos`), so water passes *through* the grain bed
continuously and pools as brew in the cup — instead of today's single fixed water column that
drains once. This is the prerequisite that unblocks real-coffee yield/TDS: a fixed column can only
strip `c_sat·V_water` (~1% of dose); continuous pour-through extracts cumulatively across many
water passes.

The design is a **port of the old implementation's emission model** (`crates/sim-wasm/src/engine/
xpbd/inflow.rs` + `crates/sim-core/src/pour.rs`, in git history), which functioned well, **re-seated
onto the new GPU-resident solver and with its bugs fixed** (see Sources & Research for the audit).
The old model was CPU-resident (particle `Vec`s grown by `push`); the new solver has fixed GPU
buffers, so emission becomes a **pre-allocated capacity pool with a growing active count**, with
particles activated CPU-side via `queue.write_buffer` into the active range.

Drained/pooled water stays **live** in the cup and yield/TDS is summed over the cup region (reusing
the Phase-1.5 U7 readout) — no per-frame readback, no atomics, no dropped water. Band calibration is
**deferred** to a follow-up tuning pass; this plan delivers the mechanism + a believable V60 pour.

---

## Problem Frame

`EmissionInput { kettle_pos, flow_rate, pour_angle, event }` exists (`src/emission/mod.rs`) but the
solver ignores it (`fn step(&mut self, dt, _input)` at `src/solvers/xpbd/mod.rs`). All GPU buffers
are sized to a fixed `particle_count` set at build from the seeded lattice, and every pass dispatches
over `groups(self.particle_count)`. There is no runtime particle injection.

Consequences (measured in Phase 1.5): the V60 brew is a one-shot water column; yield ceilings at
~4% of soluble (~1% of total dry mass) and cup TDS at ~0.99%, far under the 18–22% / 1.2–1.4% target
bands — because real yield needs 250 ml flowing *through* 15 g, not a single static charge.

The old MPM/XPBD prototype modeled this correctly (continuous spout inflow + cup drain) and "functioned
well" per the maintainer, but (a) it was CPU-resident and (b) it carries bugs that are benign in MPM
but **fatal in the new PBF hard-incompressibility solve** (a flat slab of coincident emitted particles
erupts). This plan ports the good parts and fixes the bugs.

## Requirements

- **R1 — Continuous injection.** Consume `EmissionInput.flow_rate`/`kettle_pos`/`pour_angle` to emit
  water particles over time at the kettle, at a rate matching the volumetric flow.
- **R2 — Capacity pool + active count.** Pre-allocate GPU buffers to a `capacity`; grow a runtime
  `active_count` as particles are emitted; dispatch passes over `active_count`. Non-pour scenes
  (capacity == seed count) are **byte-for-byte unchanged**.
- **R3 — PBF-safe inlet via an arclength-credit emitter (bounded local density).** Emission must not
  inject a density spike the incompressibility solve erupts on. **A per-frame axial cap does not work**
  — at realistic pour speeds (3.3–12.5 sim-units/s, KEEP.md §30) and V60 spacing 0.5, travel/frame is
  0.11–0.42·spacing, so `floor(travel/spacing) = 0` and a per-frame cap starves forever (Codex round-2).
  Instead the emitter carries an **axial-travel credit**: `axial += exit_speed·dt` each frame, and a new
  **layer** of particles is released only when `axial ≥ particle_spacing` (decrement by `particle_spacing`)
  — correct spacing at any `dt`/speed, no starvation. The layer capacity is **volume-consistent**:
  `N_layer = ceil(A_eff · particle_spacing / V_w)` where `A_eff = nozzle_area · discharge_coeff` and
  `exit_speed = flow_rate / A_eff`, so its throughput `(exit_speed/spacing)·N_layer ≥ flow_rate/V_w`
  always keeps up with the volume budget — no permanent backlog — and the disc packs to the fluid's
  **rest density** over `r_eff = sqrt(discharge_coeff)·nozzle_radius` (the PBF-safe inlet condition:
  no spike, no void). See KTD-3. *(Strengthens old bug #1.)*
- **R4 — No dose loss, no silent drop, overflow is an error for recipe scenes.** The fractional
  accumulator persists across pour pauses (cleared only on `Reset`). `requested` is **clamped to the
  free pool capacity BEFORE the accumulator is decremented** (so a transient clamp doesn't permanently
  burn dose). The emitter runs whenever `flow_rate > 0` **or backlog/accumulator can still emit**, so a
  burst carried past the end of a pour window still drains during drawdown (gating on `flow_rate > 0`
  alone would silently strand the backlog — Codex round-2). Capacity is sized to the recipe's total
  water dose; if a recipe-driven scene would overflow capacity, that is a **build/scenario error**
  surfaced loudly, not a silent warn-and-drop. Ad-hoc (non-recipe) emission still warn-clamps. *(Fixes
  old bugs #2, #3, #4 — the project rejects silent truncation.)*
- **R9 — Defined volume→particle conversion.** `EmissionInput.flow_rate` (scene units/s) and the
  recipe layer (mL/s) are reconciled by an explicit conversion: particle volume `V_w =
  particle_mass / rest_density` (simulation units), particles-per-scene-volume `= 1/V_w`, and a
  scene-units↔mL scale so `Scene.water_ml` sizes capacity in particles. The conversion is specified
  in one place and unit-tested. *(Replaces the old `PARTICLES_PER_ML` constant.)*
- **R5 — Pour recipe layer.** A time-windowed pour script (flow rate + spatial pattern: center /
  spiral / ring / point, with bloom / wait / drawdown via command presence) that a driver samples to
  produce `EmissionInput`. Ports `pour.rs`.
- **R6 — Pour-through brew.** In the V60 pour scene, water emitted at the kettle threads the bed,
  drains through the cone apex, and pools in the cup; yield (cup-region readout, U7) rises with
  continuous pour, materially above the fixed-column ~1%. Deterministic across identical runs.
- **R7 — Conservation under a source.** Emitted water (seeded `c = 0`, `f_w = 1`, `T = pour_t`) is
  accounted via an explicit running counter `total_emitted_water_mass` (+ emitted-particle count)
  maintained by `emit()`, so the gate is `total_emitted_water_mass = in-domain water +
  absorbed-into-grains` to the documented absorption-sink tolerance, not reconstructed ad hoc (Codex
  round-2). The solute inventory `Σ(grain s_f+s_s) + Σ(water c·f_w·V_w)` remains conserved by
  dissolution (emission adds only `c = 0` water, contributing no solute). The KEEP.md pour-off gate
  `bed_gain = emitted − active_water_gain ± 10%` (KEEP.md §58) is the calibration-grade form.
- **R8 (deferred)** — calibrate `k0`/`extract_rate`/`c_sat`/pour-schedule to the 18–22% / 1.2–1.4%
  bands. Out of scope here (see Scope Boundaries).

## Key Technical Decisions

### KTD-1 — Capacity pool + active count (not buffer reallocation)
GPU buffers are allocated once to `capacity = seed_count + water_dose_headroom` and never resized;
the live particle set is `active_count ∈ [seed_count, capacity]`, growing as water is emitted.
`params.particle_count` is set to `active_count` each frame (the WGSL kernels already guard
`if i >= particle_count { return }`), and host dispatches use `groups(active_count)`. **Rationale:**
the solver is GPU-resident with fixed-layout storage buffers and a ~200k perf target; reallocating
and re-binding buffers per emission is expensive and churns bind groups. A capacity pool with an
active count is the standard streaming-particle approach and matches the old model's `max_particles`
+ active count. Dormant slots `[active_count, capacity)` are never dispatched, so they need no init.
**Byte-unchanged guarantee:** a scene with no pour declares `capacity == seed_count`, so
`active_count` is constant and every existing suite is identical.

**Full active-vs-capacity audit (Codex R1 — every consumer of the old `particle_count`):**
- **`copy_buffer_to_buffer` spans must use `active_count`, not capacity.** The drag-subiter
  `vel→vel_frozen` copy, the buoyancy `vel→vel_frozen` copy, the xsph `vel_smoothed→vel` copy, and
  both dissolution/thermal `chem→chem_frozen` copies currently size as `particle_count·16`; they must
  size as `active_count·16` (buffers allocate to capacity, but only the live range is copied).
- **Dynamic pass selection.** `has_water`/`has_grain` are derived from seeded regions today. A V60
  **pour** scene seeds only grains → `has_water = false` → the water density / coupling / wetting /
  extraction passes would never run even after water is emitted. Pass selection must treat a declared
  pour as "water will be present": `has_water |= scene.declares_pour()`. (`mixed` then becomes true
  once both bed and pour exist.)
- **Live phase mirror.** Host code indexes `self.initial_phases[i]`, which has only `seed_count`
  entries — emitted water has no entry. Maintain a host-side `phases: Vec<u32>` (or a GPU phase
  readback) grown on each emission so per-particle phase is known for all `active_count` particles;
  `initial_phases` stays the reset baseline. **All consumers must switch to the mirror**, including
  `sample_extraction` AND `read_temperature` (and any other `initial_phases` indexer; Codex round-2).
- **`seed()`/`reset()`** must reset `active_count = seed_count`, write/zero only the seed-active
  ranges (not assume `particle_count == seed_count`), and restore the host phase mirror to
  `initial_phases`.

### KTD-2 — CPU-side activation via `write_buffer` (not a GPU emission kernel)
Per-frame emission counts are small (flow_rate · particles_per_volume · dt — a handful). The host
computes the requested count + positions/velocities and writes them into the active range
(`pos[active_count..active_count+requested]`, plus `vel`/`chem`/the moisture lane) with
`queue.write_buffer`, then bumps `active_count`. **Rationale:** matches the old model (CPU `push`),
needs no new compute pass or storage binding (the 8-buffer budget is tight), keeps the emission
geometry/recipe logic in testable CPU code, and a few `write_buffer`s of a handful of vec4s per frame
is negligible. A GPU kernel would only pay off at injection rates far beyond a pour.

### KTD-3 — PBF-safe inlet: a volume-consistent arclength-credit emitter
The old `emit()` placed every particle in a frame at the same `y` (`origin.y − 0.18`), spread only
across the x–z disc by a golden-angle pattern. In MPM (grid transfer, weakly compressible) this slab
was harmless; in the new PBF **hard-incompressibility** solve it is a high-risk local density spike
that can trigger the over-correction eruption the project already fought (`project_coffee_sim_rewrite`
convergence-order lesson). The fix is an emitter whose inlet density **equals the fluid's rest density
by construction** (no spike, no void), built from a flow invariant + arclength layering:

- **Orifice flow invariant via an effective flux area (ports the old `update()`):** `A_eff =
  nozzle_area · discharge_coeff` (`nozzle_area = π·nozzle_radius²`). The recipe speaks `flow_rate`
  (sim-volume/s), so the stream's `exit_speed = flow_rate / A_eff` is **derived** — `flow_rate`,
  `exit_speed`, and `A_eff` are not independent. Folding `discharge_coeff` into `A_eff` (Codex round-4)
  is what keeps the throughput algebra below exact for any `discharge_coeff ≤ 1` (the earlier draft
  silently assumed `discharge_coeff = 1`).
- **Volume budget (master count):** `accumulator += (flow_rate / V_w) · dt`; emit `floor(accumulator)`
  particles total over time (carries the fraction; backlog only sub-layer).
- **Arclength-credit layering:** `axial += exit_speed · dt`; release a layer each time `axial ≥
  particle_spacing` (decrement by `particle_spacing`). This spaces layers exactly `particle_spacing`
  apart along the stream at any `dt`/speed — no starvation (the broken `floor(exit_speed·dt/spacing)`
  per-frame cap is gone), no over-dense stacking.
- **Volume-consistent layer capacity:** each layer can hold up to `N_layer = max(1, ceil(A_eff ·
  particle_spacing / V_w))` particles — the number in a stream slab of thickness `particle_spacing`
  through the effective area. `ceil` (not `round`, Codex round-4) guarantees layer throughput
  `(exit_speed/particle_spacing)·N_layer ≥ exit_speed·A_eff/V_w = flow_rate/V_w`, so it can always
  keep up with the volume budget — **no permanent backlog**. The volume accumulator (`emit
  min(N_layer, remaining_want)` per layer) caps total emission, so the `ceil` over-capacity is simply
  unused, never over-delivered. The emitted particles fill the golden-angle disc over the **effective
  radius** `r_eff = sqrt(A_eff/π) = sqrt(discharge_coeff)·nozzle_radius` at radial spacing
  `≈ sqrt(A_eff/N_layer)`, which equals the fluid's rest spacing — the disc is packed to rest density
  (no spike, no void), consistent even when `discharge_coeff < 1`.
- **Domain-clear placement:** layers spawn strictly inside the domain / above the bed, never on the
  box clamp.

Enforce + test the throughput invariant (U2): `(exit_speed/particle_spacing)·N_layer ≥ flow_rate/V_w`
must hold — a test fails if a recipe's requested flow exceeds layer throughput (which `A_eff` + `ceil`
guarantee it never does). Empirical PBF stability is still gated by the full-solve inlet sweep (U2).
Keep the deterministic cursor + golden-angle disc.

### KTD-4 — Drain = pool live + cup-region readout (not CPU banking)
Poured water that reaches the cup stays **live** (it settles and pools); yield/TDS is summed over the
cup-cylinder region by the existing Phase-1.5 U7 `sample_extraction`. **Rationale:** the old model
banked drained mass/solute into CPU scalars because it was CPU-resident; on the GPU-resident solver
that would require a per-frame readback of just-drained particles (a realtime stall) or an
atomics-free reduction. Pooling live needs neither, reuses code already written, and never drops
water. **Cost (Codex R4 — not free by assertion):** `capacity` must hold the full water dose, so
`active_count` rises monotonically to the whole recipe dose, increasing grid-scatter size, neighbor
gather cost, and cup-pool neighbor density — pooled cup water is *low-velocity* (grain-sleep regime)
but is still dispatched, sorted, and gathered every frame, so "quiescent" is not "free". The plan
therefore sets capacity as an explicit **particle budget** = `seed_count + ceil(water_ml / 5.20 /
V_w)` (KTD-7). **Pinned for the V60 scene** (spacing 0.5, 250 mL): ≈ 250/5.20/V_w ≈ a few hundred
water particles + the ~17-grain bed ⇒ a low-hundreds-to-~1k capacity — trivially under the deferred
~200k perf target, so the V60 pour is comfortably affordable (the worry is correctness, not cost, at
this scale). U4 prints **max occupancy + per-frame dispatch time** and asserts capacity is never hit.
If a future finer-resolution or larger scene pushes the dose-pool past the perf budget, the fallback
is the deferred drain-and-reclaim path (Scope Boundaries) — flagged, not built here.

### KTD-5 — Recipe layer drives `EmissionInput`; the solver owns the inflow accumulator
`emission/pour.rs` (ported `PourScript`) is sampled by the driver (example/engine) at the brew time
to produce `EmissionInput`; the solver holds the `InflowState` (fractional accumulator + cursor) and
turns `EmissionInput` into activated particles in `emit()`. **Rationale:** preserves the solver-agnostic
seam (`EmissionInput` is the contract; the recipe is solver-independent) and mirrors the old split
(`pour.rs` recipe vs `inflow.rs` mechanism).

### KTD-6 — Emission seeds the chem/moisture lanes consistently
Emitted water is written with `phase = 0` (water), moisture lane `pos.w = 1.0` (`f_w = 1`, full),
and `chem = (0, pour_t, 0, 0)` (`c = 0`, water temperature = pour temperature). **Rationale:** matches
`seed_chem` for water; emitting at `c = 0` means emission adds no solute, so the U5/U6 conservation
gates are undisturbed (emission only adds dilution/volume). The old hardcoded `92.0` temperature is
replaced by `mats.pour_t`.

### KTD-7 — One defined volume→particle conversion, pinned to KEEP.md (Codex R6)
The recipe (`pour.rs`) speaks mL/s; the solver works in sim units; the old code bridged with a
`PARTICLES_PER_ML` constant. **Pinned constants (KEEP.md §27):** `ML_PER_SIM_UNIT3 = 5.20` (volume
scale). Particle volume `V_w = particle_mass / rest_density` (sim-unit³; `water_particle_volume()`
already exposes it), so **particles-per-sim-unit³ = 1/V_w** and **particles-per-mL = 1/(V_w · 5.20)**.
`EmissionInput.flow_rate` is redefined/documented as **sim-volume per second** (not the current vague
"scene units/s"); the recipe's mL/s converts via `/5.20`. `Scene.water_ml / 5.20 / V_w` gives the
capacity dose in particles (KTD-4). **Rationale:** one conversion, pinned to the project's existing
unit table and unit-tested; removes the ambiguity Codex flagged. The accumulator (KTD-3/U2) integrates
`flow_rate / V_w` (particles/s). Concretely at the V60 calibrated mats (spacing 0.5): 250 mL / 5.20 /
V_w ≈ a few hundred water particles — see KTD-4 for the pinned budget.

## High-Level Technical Design

Per-frame pour pipeline (driver → solver):

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  PS["PourScript.sample(t)\n(emission/pour.rs)"] -->|x,y,flow| DRV["driver maps to\nEmissionInput\n{kettle_pos, flow_rate}"]
  DRV --> STEP["XpbdSolver::step(dt, input)"]
  STEP --> EMIT["emit(): InflowState\naccumulator + golden-angle\n+ y-stagger (KTD-3)"]
  EMIT -->|write_buffer slots\n[active_count .. +req]| POOL[("GPU pool\nactive_count→")]
  EMIT -->|active_count += req\nparams.particle_count = active_count| SUB["substep loop\n(dispatch over active_count)"]
  SUB --> DRAIN["water pools in cup\n(stays live)"]
  DRAIN --> READ["sample_extraction()\ncup-region yield/TDS (U7)"]
```

Capacity / active-count model (KTD-1):

```
build:   capacity = seed_count + dose_headroom        # dose_headroom = 0 for non-pour scenes
         buffers sized to capacity
         active_count = seed_count                     # bed (+ any initial charge)
per frame: requested = inflow.emit(input, dt)          # fractional accumulator → integer count
           requested = min(requested, capacity - active_count)   # clamp + WARN if clamped (R4)
           write pos/vel/chem/moisture for [active_count, active_count+requested)
           active_count += requested
           params.particle_count = active_count        # kernels guard i >= particle_count
           run substep loop over groups(active_count)
```

- **Multi-solver emission** — only the XPBD solver; the `EmissionInput` seam stays solver-agnostic.

## Implementation Units

### U1. Capacity pool + active-count refactor

**Goal:** Let the solver hold more particles than it currently simulates — fixed-capacity GPU buffers
with a runtime `active_count` — without changing behavior for non-pour scenes, and with every
consumer of the old fixed `particle_count` audited (Codex R1).

**Requirements:** R2, R9 (capacity sizing uses the conversion).
**Dependencies:** none.
**Files:** `src/solvers/xpbd/mod.rs`, `src/engine/scene.rs` (a `declares_pour()` / water-dose source),
`src/models/mod.rs` or `src/utils/config.rs` (the mL↔scene-units scale, KTD-7), a new
`tests/xpbd_emission.rs` (byte-unchanged + inert-capacity assertions).

**Approach:** Introduce `capacity` (buffer sizing) distinct from `active_count` (live set). Size all
per-particle buffers (`pos`, `vel`, `pred`, `vel_smoothed`, `vel_frozen`, `lambda`, `dp`, `c_residual`,
`normal_impulse`, `alpha_s`, `fluid_impulse`, `coupling_scale`, `wet_count`, `diss_neighbors`, `chem`,
`chem_frozen`, `phase`, `sorted_indices`, the vec4 readback) to `capacity`. Replace `self.particle_count`
with `active_count` at every use:
- dispatch sizing (`groups(active_count)`),
- `params.particle_count = active_count` before each params upload,
- **every `copy_buffer_to_buffer` span** (`vel→vel_frozen` ×2, `vel_smoothed→vel`, `chem→chem_frozen`
  ×2) sized `active_count·16`, not `particle_count·16`,
- the host **phase mirror** (`Vec<u32>` grown on emission) used by `sample_extraction` instead of the
  fixed-length `initial_phases`.

Derive `capacity` at build: `seed_count + ceil(water_ml · particles_per_ml)` via the KTD-7 conversion
— **0 dose-headroom when the scene declares no pour** (`capacity == active_count == seed_count`,
layout/dispatch identical to today). **Pass selection:** `has_water |= scene.declares_pour()` so a
grain-only pour scene still runs the water/coupling/wetting/extraction passes (Codex R1). Grid sizing
(`num_cells`) is box-derived and unaffected; `cell_start` stays `num_cells+1`; `sorted_indices` grows
to `capacity`. `seed()`/`reset()` set `active_count = seed_count`, write only the seed range, and
restore the phase mirror to `initial_phases`.

**Patterns to follow:** the buffer-allocation block + `let vec4 = n*16` sizing and the
`copy_buffer_to_buffer` call sites in `XpbdSolver::build`/`step`; `seed_block` for the seed count;
`has_water`/`has_grain` derivation; the `groups(self.particle_count)` dispatch sites.

**Test scenarios:**
- Existing `xpbd_water`/`xpbd_bed`/`xpbd_wetting`/`xpbd_coupling`/`xpbd_geometry`/`xpbd_extraction`
  suites stay green unchanged (non-pour scenes ⇒ `capacity == active_count == seed_count`). Covers R2.
- A solver built with `capacity > seed_count` but `active_count == seed_count` (no emission) produces
  results identical to `capacity == seed_count` — dormant slots are provably inert.
- `reset()` returns `active_count` to `seed_count` and restores the phase mirror.
- A grain-only scene with a declared pour has `has_water == true` (pass-selection regression).

**Verification:** all existing GPU suites pass unchanged; dormant capacity inert; pour scenes select
the water passes.

### U2. Emission core — `InflowState` + PBF-safe staggered injection

**Goal:** Turn `EmissionInput` into activated water particles each frame, ported from the old
`inflow.rs` with the density-spike and dose-loss bugs fixed.

**Requirements:** R1, R3, R4, R6 (determinism), R7 (emission side), R9.
**Dependencies:** U1.
**Files:** `src/solvers/xpbd/mod.rs` (the `InflowState` + `emit()` + `step()` wiring), possibly a new
`src/solvers/xpbd/inflow.rs` (host module) for the accumulator/geometry, `src/emission/mod.rs` or
`src/utils/config.rs` (spout settings: `nozzle_radius`, `discharge_coeff` — port the old
`SpoutSettings`), `tests/xpbd_emission.rs`.

**Approach:** Port `InflowState` (volume accumulator + axial-travel credit + `cursor`) as host code.
`emit(input, dt)`:
0. **Orifice invariant (effective area):** `A_eff = π·nozzle_radius² · discharge_coeff`; `exit_speed =
   flow_rate / A_eff` (ports the old `update()`; folding `discharge_coeff` into `A_eff` keeps step-2
   throughput exactly matched to the step-1 budget for any `discharge_coeff ≤ 1`).
1. **Volume budget:** `accumulator += (flow_rate / V_w) · dt` (KTD-7; `flow_rate` is sim-volume/s);
   `want = floor(accumulator)` — the master particle-count budget (carries the fraction; never reset
   on zero flow, only on `Reset`) [fixes bug #2].
2. **Arclength-credit layering (R3/KTD-3 — replaces the broken per-frame floor):** `axial +=
   exit_speed·dt`. While `axial ≥ particle_spacing` AND budget remains, release a **layer**:
   `axial -= particle_spacing`, emit `min(N_layer, remaining_want)` particles where the
   **volume-consistent** `N_layer = max(1, ceil(A_eff · particle_spacing / V_w))` (`ceil`, not round,
   so throughput `≥ flow_rate/V_w` — Codex round-4). The volume accumulator caps total emission, so the
   `ceil` slack is unused not over-delivered → backlog stays sub-layer; the disc packs to fluid rest
   density over `r_eff = sqrt(discharge_coeff)·nozzle_radius`. `emit_n` = total released this frame.
3. **Clamp to free pool BEFORE decrementing (R4):** `emit_n = min(emit_n, capacity − active_count)`;
   subtract only the emitted count from `accumulator`. Recipe-scene capacity overflow → hard
   build/scenario error; ad-hoc emission warn-clamps.
4. For each emitted particle: golden-angle disc offset (deterministic `cursor`; disc radius `r_eff =
   sqrt(discharge_coeff)·nozzle_radius`, fill spacing ≈ `particle_spacing`) at its layer depth: `pos =
   kettle_pos + dir·layer_depth + disc_offset`, `dir` from `pour_angle` (downward default), spawned
   strictly inside the domain.
5. `write_buffer` the new slots: `pos` (`.w = 1.0`), `vel` (= `dir·exit_speed`), `chem = (0, pour_t,
   0, 0)`, `phase = 0` [KTD-6]; push `phase = 0` onto the host phase mirror (U1); add
   `emit_n · particle_mass` to `total_emitted_water_mass` (R7).
6. `active_count += emit_n`; update `params`.
Wire `emit()` into `step()` gated on `flow_rate > 0 OR backlog pending` (so end-of-window backlog
drains during drawdown — not `flow_rate > 0` alone, which would strand it) [R4]. `PourEvent::Reset`
re-seeds + clears accumulator/backlog/credit; `StartBloom`/`StopBloom` get a minimal flow-scale hook
(stub).

**Execution note:** Add the failing PBF-stability test (emit into a closed box, assert no eruption)
before wiring `emit()` — it is the load-bearing fix.

**Patterns to follow:** the old `crates/sim-wasm/src/engine/xpbd/inflow.rs` `emit()` (accumulator +
golden angle); `write_velocities_for_test`/`write_chem_for_test` for the `write_buffer` mechanics;
`seed_chem` for the water chem lanes.

**Test scenarios:**
- **Rate fidelity at realistic pour speeds (gate against starvation):** constant `flow_rate` at gentle
  (0.12 m/s ≈ 3.3 sim-u/s) AND high (0.45 m/s ≈ 12.5 sim-u/s) pours, V60 spacing 0.5, for T s →
  activates `≈ flow_rate/V_w · T` particles (±1); crucially the gentle pour does **not** starve (the
  old per-frame floor would emit 0). Covers R1/R3/R9.
- **Arclength layering spaces particles:** consecutive axial layers are ≥ ~`particle_spacing` apart and
  within-layer particles ≈ `particle_spacing` apart (no over-dense stream), independent of `dt`.
  Covers R3.
- **Throughput invariant (no permanent backlog):** for the recipe's `flow_rate`/`nozzle`/spacing,
  assert `(exit_speed/particle_spacing)·N_layer ≥ flow_rate/V_w` (equality under the orifice relation);
  run a long constant pour and assert backlog stays sub-layer (does not grow unboundedly) and the
  emitted count tracks `flow_rate/V_w·T`. Fails if requested flow exceeds layer throughput. Covers
  R1/R3/R9.
- **Accumulator + backlog persist + drain in drawdown:** flow→0 mid-window then resumes (no loss); a
  burst exceeding one frame's layer capacity meters across frames; **backlog carried past a pour
  window's end still drains during drawdown** (the gate is `flow>0 || backlog`). Total matches the
  integral (±1). Covers R4.
- **PBF-safe inlet sweep (gate):** continuous emission into a closed box with the full solve on, swept
  over `flow_rate × dt × exit_speed × nozzle_radius` → positions finite, max speed + KE bounded, no
  eruption; an over-dense (single-layer-slab) variant visibly spikes (negative control). Covers R3.
- **Overflow semantics:** ad-hoc emission past `capacity` warn-clamps with exact count; a recipe scene
  that under-declares capacity raises the build/scenario error (not a silent drop). Covers R4.
- **Seeding + emitted-mass counter:** emitted particles read back `phase = 0`, `pos.w = 1.0`,
  `chem.x = 0`, `chem.y = pour_t`; the host phase mirror length == `active_count`; and
  `total_emitted_water_mass == emitted_count · particle_mass`. Covers R6/R7.
- **Determinism:** identical pour inputs → identical activated positions/velocities across two runs.

**Verification:** rate matches flow; no eruption across the inlet sweep; backlog conserves dose; no
silent drops; deterministic.

### U4. V60 pour scene + driver + believable-brew gate

**Goal:** Wire the recipe → `EmissionInput` → solver into a runnable V60 pour, and gate the
pour-through brew (conservation, stability, rising yield).

**Requirements:** R6, R7 (+ exercises R4 overflow-as-error and the KTD-4 perf budget).
**Dependencies:** U1, U2, U3.
**Files:** `src/engine/scene.rs` (`Scene::v60_pour()` — bed seeded, no initial water column, declares
the water dose for capacity sizing), `examples/brew.rs` (extend) or `examples/pour.rs` (new) for the
driver loop, `tests/xpbd_emission.rs` (integration gates), reuse `sample_extraction` from U7.

**Approach:** `Scene::v60_pour()` reuses the V60 dripper/cup geometry + the calibrated permeable bed,
seeds only grains (water arrives via the pour), and declares the total water dose so U1 sizes capacity.
A driver loop samples a `PourScript` at the brew time → maps `(x,y)` to `kettle_pos` above the bed →
sets `EmissionInput { kettle_pos, flow_rate, pour_angle }` → `step()`. Print yield/TDS (U7 readout) +
conservation (emitted mass vs in-domain + absorbed) + `active_count` / max occupancy / per-frame
dispatch time over the brew. Bloom command pours center first; main pour follows; drawdown = no command.
Size the scene's dose so the full-dose pool stays within a stated **particle budget** (KTD-4); if the
chosen V60 dose would exceed it, scale the scene down (fewer ml / coarser spacing) rather than blowing
the budget — and document the budget number.

**Test scenarios:**
- **Pour-through (gate):** over a full pour, water emitted at the kettle reaches the cup (cup-region
  water mass rises from 0), grains stay trapped, and the run stays finite/stable. Covers R6.
- **Yield rises with pour (gate):** cumulative pour yields materially above the fixed-column ceiling
  (~1% of total) and increases over the brew — the qualitative win the mechanism unlocks. Covers R6.
- **Conservation under a source (gate):** the explicit `total_emitted_water_mass` counter (R7) equals
  in-domain water + absorbed-into-grains within the absorption-sink tolerance — i.e. KEEP.md's pour-off
  form `bed_gain = emitted − active_water_gain ± 10%` (KEEP.md §58); solute inventory conserved
  (emission adds `c = 0`). Covers R7.
- **Capacity is honored, never overflowed:** `active_count` reaches the declared dose without ever
  hitting `capacity` (no overflow error fires) — the scene declares enough capacity. Covers R4.
- **Perf budget:** `active_count` at full pour ≤ the stated particle budget, and per-frame dispatch
  time stays within the V60 budget (a soft gate / printed metric, not a hard CI assert). Covers KTD-4.
- **Determinism:** identical pour script → aggregate metrics within tolerance across two runs.

**Verification:** water pours through to the cup; yield rises beyond the fixed-column ceiling;
conservation holds; capacity honored; within the perf budget; stable; example prints a believable trace.

## Risks & Dependencies

- **Inlet instability (highest risk).** Density safety comes from the volume-consistent arclength-credit
  emitter (KTD-3): layers spaced one `particle_spacing` apart, each packed to the fluid rest density over
  `r_eff` — so the inlet matches rest density by construction (no spike, no void). U2 still gates this
  empirically with a full-solve sweep over flow / dt / exit_speed / nozzle. If it still erupts at extreme
  flow, the lever is a shorter `dt`/substep or a wider nozzle (lower exit_speed) — flagged for
  execution-time tuning.
- **Capacity sizing + overflow.** KTD-4 pools live, so `capacity` must hold the full dose. Overflow on
  a recipe scene is a hard build/scenario error (R4), not a silent under-delivery; the V60 dose is
  sized within the particle budget (KTD-4) and U4 asserts `active_count` never reaches `capacity`.
- **Perf (not free).** Active-count rises to the full dose; pooled cup water is low-velocity but still
  dispatched/sorted/gathered, and dense cup-neighbor loops cost. KTD-4 sets an explicit particle
  budget and U4 prints occupancy + dispatch time; the ~200k perf pass and drain-and-reclaim are
  deferred levers.
- **Unit conversion (Codex R6).** The mL↔scene-units↔particles chain (KTD-7) must be defined once and
  unit-tested before U2, or rate fidelity and capacity sizing are guesswork.
- **Conservation tolerance.** The wetting absorption sink (Phase 1.5) already makes the combined-path
  inventory drift ~1–2%; emission adds only `c = 0` water, but the gate must use the same bounded
  tolerance (applied after the source), not assert zero.

## Sources & Research

**Old emission model (git history; the reference the maintainer confirmed "functioned well"):**
- `crates/sim-wasm/src/engine/xpbd/inflow.rs` (last at commit `6631684`) — `InflowState` fractional
  accumulator + golden-angle disc emission; consumed in `crates/sim-wasm/src/engine/xpbd/mod.rs`
  `emit()` (capacity pool + `max_particles` + `dropped`) and `drain_cup()` (cup-floor deactivate +
  CPU mass/solute banking).
- `crates/sim-core/src/pour.rs` (pre-deletion `bfe129e^`) — `PourScript` recipe layer + patterns +
  built-in recipes.

**Bug audit of the old model (carried into requirements as fixes):**
1. **Pancake emission → density-spike eruption (high risk for PBF).** All per-frame particles at the
   same `y`; harmless in MPM, a likely eruption trigger in PBF's hard-incompressibility solve. The
   sub-frame stagger is *necessary but not a proof* — it must be paired with the bounded-density
   criterion + burst backlog and validated empirically. → R3 / KTD-3.
2. **Accumulator reset on flow-stop** (`accumulator = 0.0` when flow < 1e-6) → lost sub-particle dose
   per pause. → R4 (persist the fraction).
3. **Drain never reclaims the slot** (`pos_type[i][3] = 2.0` but stays in the array) → `active_count`
   only grows → eventual cap + silent drop. → KTD-4 (pool live, size capacity to dose) + R4. Note:
   pooling live does not *eliminate* capacity pressure — it converts it into bounded active-count
   growth + dense-cup cost, budgeted explicitly in KTD-4/U4 (drain-and-reclaim is the deferred lever).
4. **Silent drop on full pool** (`dropped` tracked but water silently lost). → R4 (loud warn, no drop).
5. **Hardcoded pour temp `92.0` + y-offset `0.18`** in `emit()`. → KTD-6 (`pour_t`, geometry-derived).
6. **"Spiral" is a rosette** (radius locked to angle frequency). → U3 (fix or document; cosmetic).
- **Kept (good):** fractional accumulator, deterministic cursor + golden-angle spread, the `pour.rs`
  recipe layer.

**New-solver context:** `src/solvers/xpbd/mod.rs` (fixed `particle_count`, `groups(particle_count)`
dispatch, buffer sizing `n*16`); `src/emission/mod.rs` (`EmissionInput`/`PourEvent` scaffold);
Phase-1.5 U7 `sample_extraction` cup-region yield/TDS readout (reused for the drain readout);
`project_coffee_sim_rewrite` memory (the convergence-order eruption lesson that makes KTD-3 load-bearing).
