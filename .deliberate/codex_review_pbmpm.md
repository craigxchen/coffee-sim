REVISE

The direction is plausible, but the doc is not yet sound as an architecture decision. The repo supports the diagnosis that two-field is soft and density-loophole-prone; it does not support the stronger claims that PB-MPM gives stiff incompressibility at the same few-iteration budget, avoids global pressure work, and yields a pore-pressure field just by reusing `lambda`.

Major findings:

1. The two-field diagnosis mostly checks out. [pressure.wgsl](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/src/solvers/twofield/pressure.wgsl:95) explicitly says the projection constrains velocity divergence, not accumulated density; [cell_classify](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/src/solvers/twofield/pressure.wgsl:482) adds density relief as an RHS source; [surface.wgsl](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/src/solvers/twofield/surface.wgsl:175) adds the under-density half separately. The comments at [pressure.wgsl:159](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/src/solvers/twofield/pressure.wgsl:159) directly document the under-converged local-Jacobi/density-relief/APIC limit cycle. So rejecting “keep patching the current `∇·v` projection” is reasonable.

2. The “PB-MPM escapes the Poisson trap” claim is overstated. PB-MPM/PBD can give stable local push-back and likely visible bounce at low iteration counts, but it does not magically provide globally stiff 3D incompressibility. The EA PB-MPM paper says PBD behavior depends on convergence and Jacobi-style parallel constraint solving has slow convergence/artificial softness; it also presents stability, not exact stiffness, as the win. KD2’s “coarse-grid pressure pre-pass” is therefore in tension with “no global Poisson.” A coarse helper may still be cheaper than a full fine-grid Poisson, but the doc must define it as an approximate global low-frequency solve and measure it, not treat “not a full Poisson” as self-evident.

3. KD5 is the deepest physics over-claim. XPBD does not derive buoyancy from a smooth `∇λ` field. In [xpbd/coupling.wgsl](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/src/solvers/xpbd/coupling.wgsl:228), `pressure_for_water(i) = max(-lambda[i], 0)`, then buoyancy is pairwise SPH-gradient accumulation over neighboring water/grain pairs at [coupling.wgsl:232](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/src/solvers/xpbd/coupling.wgsl:232) and [coupling.wgsl:274](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/src/solvers/xpbd/coupling.wgsl:274). That is not a cell-centered pore-pressure gradient suitable for Terzaghi “exactly as XPBD.” A PB-MPM multiplier may be usable as an algorithmic pressure only after sign, units, smoothing, iteration-dependence, and stress-partition audits. The prior 2026-06-17 doc correctly warned that PBF `lambda` is particle-side, compression-negative, and not directly portable to the grid pressure contract.

4. The perf anchor is useful but not fully apples-to-apples. Two-field’s 25.4 ms gate is strong: [PERF_NOTES.md](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/docs/PERF_NOTES.md:62) and [tests/twofield_perf.rs](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/tests/twofield_perf.rs:1) measure a full coupled saturated-pour scene at ~207k. XPBD’s ~90 ms is extrapolated from water-only dam-break/scaling notes with optimization levers still open in [PERF_NOTES.md](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/docs/PERF_NOTES.md:10). It proves “grid is faster today,” not that particle XPBD is fairly eliminated as an architecture. Also, KD8 under-tests: a single-phase PB-MPM water prototype passing ≤33 ms does not bound two velocity fields, drag, bed plasticity, wetting, extra buffers, and extra dispatches.

5. The old `main` Poisson-cost anchor is plausible but should be cited more carefully. From `origin/main`, the old MPM uses the volume-projection target at `crates/sim-wasm/src/mpm_3d/shader.rs:765`, RBGS pressure update at `:1766`, and 40-100 red/black pressure pairs in `mod.rs`. That makes “pressure dominates frame” plausible. I did not find a checked-in profiler table proving exactly “~70%,” so the doc should cite the render/profiler artifact or soften the number.

6. Constraint feasibility is under-weighted. [AGENTS.md](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/AGENTS.md:33) now allows a 16-storage-buffer per-stage ceiling, but [gpu.rs](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/src/utils/gpu.rs:108) still requests exactly 9, and two-field’s internal widest-pass budget is 7 in [common.wgsl](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/src/solvers/twofield/common.wgsl:9). PB-MPM can maybe fit by pass splitting and packing, but the doc needs a real buffer/pass ledger. Fixed-point is also not a footnote: the current FP headroom math is for mass/momentum lanes in [common.wgsl](/Users/cxc/Github/coffee-sim/src/solvers/twofield/common.wgsl:124), not accumulated position corrections or PB-MPM displacement state.

7. Candidate evaluation skips a serious lower-risk candidate. The 2026-06-17 brainstorm proposed a compliant predicted-density-error projection on the existing two-field operator, preserving the cell-centered pressure contract. That is not the same as “keep patching `∇·v`.” If the new doc supersedes it, it must explain why full two-velocity PB-MPM is worth rebuilding coupling despite that prior doc’s strongest objection: full PB-MPM produces position/displacement corrections, while the bed coupling consumes `G·p`.

8. Requirements need sharper gates. R1 can be gamed by noisy upward motion; require a localized post-impact rebound/spread metric with cap-hit/popcorn/conservation guards. R3’s “two-field fidelity” is too vague; make drawdown, saturation, grain seating, stress partition, and conserved absorption explicit. R4 should include density histogram/volume and rebound criteria, not only “reads incompressible.”

Specific REVISE items:

- Rewrite KD1/KD2 to say PB-MPM is expected to improve stable local stiffness, not guarantee globally stiff incompressibility at few iterations; define the coarse pre-pass and admit it is global low-frequency pressure work.
- Downgrade KD5 from assertion to hypothesis: “PB-MPM multiplier may be converted into a pore-pressure-like field if validated,” with required sign/unit/smoothness/Terzaghi audits.
- Correct the XPBD citation: XPBD coupling uses pairwise SPH-gradient impulses from `max(-lambda,0)`, not a grid `∇λ` pressure field.
- Add candidate D: compliant density projection on the existing two-field operator, and explicitly justify why full PB-MPM beats it.
- Expand KD8 to include a two-velocity cost projection or stub prototype with drag, extra fields, λ/ping-pong, and bed/wetting buffer budget.
- Add a storage-buffer ledger against both current requested 9 and sanctioned 16, plus a required `GpuContext` limit-change gate if relying on >9.
- Add fixed-point/displacement headroom gates for PB-MPM position corrections and any grid accumulation lanes.
- Sharpen R1/R3/R4 into non-gameable metrics with conservation, cap-hit, density, and settled-tail guards.
- Remove or cite the exact “main Poisson ~70% of frame” profiler source; otherwise soften it to “pressure-pair loop plausibly dominates.”
- Remove “publishable core” rhetoric unless the document clearly separates validated repo physics from speculative research contribution.
